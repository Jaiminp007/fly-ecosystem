# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-13

Initial published research baseline: compact evolving neural agents, two-parent
inheritance, individual plasticity, structural mutation, energy-accounted habitat,
local visualization and checkpoints. Includes automated model/API tests,
reproducible treatment experiments, separate connectome assay and a documented
long-run extinction investigation. Always-on authenticated hosting is planned,
not part of this release.
