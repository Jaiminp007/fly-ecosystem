What does “learning” mean here?

The compact model changes connection strengths during each life in response to food gained and energy spent. Children inherit genomes and start with fresh acquired state. Founders have an engineered food-seeking reflex. Changing weights or surviving longer alone does not demonstrate useful learning. The separate MaleCNS connectome runtime remains a distinct model.
