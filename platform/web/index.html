What this experiment measures

Agents have recurrent artificial neural controllers. Food, danger, nearby agents, and internal state feed their inputs. Outputs govern movement, feeding, mating, and a simple signal. Eligible pairs transfer energy to children that inherit genome traits and network structure, with optional mutation. Children start with fresh neural activity and learned weight changes.

These first habitats use the existing compact model and its engineered founder reflex. The full MaleCNS fruit-fly model remains available through its separate entry point. Neither model reproduces a human brain. Survival, network growth, and changing weights alone do not establish useful learning or intelligence.
