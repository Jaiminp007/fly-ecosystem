# Native-action certificate: safe observations, costly conservative estimate

The fixed certificate never contradicted a recorded action on the 28,090
full/residual-only transfer decisions; it certified native on 5,985/14,080
full-model decisions and 1,734/14,010 residual-only decisions. All residual-only
certificates came from the wall guard; its all-17-context numerical bound did
not certify any additional decision. Synthetic boundary, tie, invalid-observation
and randomized tests also pass. This is evidence on those inputs, not a formal
floating-point verification or an embodied controller.

The specified conservative same-state estimate credits only guaranteed
observation, selector and guard work that a certificate would skip, while
charging certificate work on every decision, clearing operations and persistent
proof scratch byte-time. Under that accounting the estimated cost **increases**
by 0.605 energy across all full-model forks and 4.071 across residual-only
forks. This deliberately excludes potential additional savings such as point
storage, so it is not a measurement of actual net energy and does not prove
that every implementation would lose. The certificate is not integrated.

For the two previously selected residual-only focal bodies, the same
conservative estimate is -0.118 and -0.020 energy on their respective observed
lifetimes, with 112 and 38 wall proofs. These are post-outcome same-state
calculations, not evidence of rescue or a reason to waive costs. All 16
captured-source physical replays exactly match their original checkpoints and
trace hashes. Protocol, captured sources, per-founder counts and results are
in `evidence/native-steering-certificate/20261026-v1`.

The numerical all-context check is expensive when it cannot prove a margin.
Any simpler wall-only or short-circuit implementation is a new outcome-informed
engineering candidate and needs separately fixed accounting and physical
controls. No transfer, survival, functional-pressure or qualification gate has
passed; closed reserves remain closed.
