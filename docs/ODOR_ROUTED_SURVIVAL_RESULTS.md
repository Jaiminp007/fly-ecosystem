# Frozen-versus-continuing survival census

All eight learned and frozen-after-A acquisition traces have identical recorded
events through tick 512. At A/B/C/D boundaries, continuing populations have
192/186/30/20 survivors, while frozen populations have 192/185/69/60. Most of
the survival gap therefore appears during scarce cue phase C, before the
odor-free acquisition phase. The independent readback checks 545,684
decision/receipt pairs, the eight A-prefix hashes and lineage/phase aggregation.

After the shared A phase, the arms encounter different trajectories and
survival. In B and C, continuously learning bodies tend to move faster, while
frozen bodies spend more decisions stationary; actual food exposure, toxin and
physical costs differ. In odor-free D, frozen bodies execute full speed on all
16,139 observed decisions, while continuing bodies execute 947 stationary,
1,758 half-speed and 3,393 full-speed decisions. Controller energy paid over
the complete populations is approximately 727.59 versus 732.12 units; differing
lifetimes and work make this aggregate unsuitable as a simple per-body cost
comparison. The result does not isolate learning, exploration, action choices,
resource competition or interaction-driven RNG divergence.

Raw phase totals and per-history outcomes are preserved in
`evidence/odor-routed-survival-census/20261026-v1`. This is outcome-informed
development evidence, not qualification or an independent physical simulation.

## Fixed component intervention

For each of the eight actually recorded learned A checkpoints, freeze only both
routed motor banks at the A-to-B boundary. Keep the taste learner and steering
learner acquiring from their own actual experiences, preserve all other state,
and charge the motor-freeze setup and full encoded state. Continue B/C/D using
the original development seeds without refill. Compare the resulting world with
the already recorded continuously learning and frozen-after-A arms, whose A
event prefixes match. Save source/input hashes, all continuation traces and
checkpoints and retain every dead founder's debt.

This fixed post-outcome intervention changes motor updates, motor exploration
and their computational work together. It is a total-world diagnostic, not a
pure gradient-only effect: future food competition, behavior and RNG consumption
can change. It is not a parameter sweep, a learner training label, fresh
qualification, or proof of retention/transfer/RSI. The unchanged controls and
gates remain mandatory.
