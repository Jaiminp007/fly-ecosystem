# GAS TRAJECTORY V1: alarm yes, plateau no — hub deficit is set early, then compounds

Analytic-only. Zero new worlds, zero new seeds, zero bank draws.
Executes the R125-specified GAS analytic on 396 frozen mean-energy curves
(60 paired hub−spread same-seed rows).
Script: `scripts/gas-trajectory.py` (+ inline gap/shape follow-ups, same data).

## Question

Do hub time-courses show Selye's alarm → resistance → exhaustion, with a
flat despotic-holding (resistance) phase? Pre-registered rule: SUPPORTED
iff hub shows all three phases relative to spread; else NOT SUPPORTED.

## Findings (paired same-seed hub−spread, n=60)

1. **Same start, differential alarm.** Tick 1–50 energy equal (med diff
   −0.06); early slope (1–256) steeper in hub 54/60 (−0.036 vs −0.025
   per tick). The deficit is GENERATED, not endowed — alarm SUPPORTED.
2. **Gap −2.1 by tick 256 → −10.2 by run end; same sign 46/60.**
   Direction is set in the alarm window; magnitude COMPOUNDS ~5× after
   it. Not parallel tracks — a poverty trap (lower base → same-shaped
   decline from less → wider gap).
3. **Shape identity, not distinct syndrome.** Mid/run slope ÷ early
   slope: hub 0.557 vs spread 0.590 — the SAME decline shape at
   different levels. Hubs do not run different dynamics; they run the
   same dynamics poorer. No flat resistance plateau exists in EITHER arm
   (mid decline continues at ~0.56× early rate throughout).
4. **Late flattening + slight recovery in both arms; zero extinctions.**
   No line ever dies (tEnd=1792 everywhere) — turnover replaces the
   exhausted. Exhaustion is individual-only (100% energetic deaths,
   MORTALITY round), never systemic.

## Verdict (measurement — GAS re-read 1-of-3)

- **SUPPORTED:** alarm (differential early overspend sets the hub deficit).
- **NOT SUPPORTED:** resistance-as-plateau (replaced by compounding-deficit
  dynamics — worse than GAS: no holding phase, just widening) and
  exhaustion-as-extinction at line level (turnover prevents it).
- **LICENSED:** the alarm window (first ~256 ticks) as the origin of hub
  fate — any rescue instrument (topology/attraction/transplant timing)
  must act there; late-line interventions fight a 5×-compounded gap.
  Pairs the rescue-transplant direction (R107) with a clock.
- **NOT LICENSED:** plateau-holding accounts of despotism (hubs hold
  nothing — they decline in parallel, poorer); any claim that lines
  collapse (they don't — individuals do, lines turn over).
- No bank, instrument, or verdict rescored. Cumulative worlds unchanged.
