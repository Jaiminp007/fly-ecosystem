# Component mortality and cost census

The residual-only intervention beats the paid native architecture on energy in
7/8 reused histories, yet the post-outcome comparison to cheap native-full
loses two aggregate survivors and wins only 3/8 on energy. Before changing a
learner, trace every founder that entered these matched transfer forks in the
recorded residual-only, full, matched native and cheap native-full worlds.
This is a retrospective descriptive census, not an additional performance
sample, fixed-neighbor causal estimate or an acquired-skill claim.

Verify the same starting physical world, source/input and trace hashes. From
actual decision/physical receipts, reconstruct each founder's decisions,
steering, energy, food, toxin, total cost and paid extra, plus the last twelve
decisions and receipts. Match each final survivor by identity. For extinct
founders, report the last receipt and distinguish a positive-energy age death
from energy depletion according to the engine's actual death rule; inspect the
recorded age/lifespan and do not infer causation from temporal correlation.
Retain initial and terminal unpaid debt. Compare survivor discordances and
expenses only on the original shared bodies; different trajectories and
populations do not imply independent individual effects.

No new outcome is fed into a learner. Do not use the two aggregate losses to
assume every discordant founder was harmed by its own steering, or tune an
energy threshold. If a specific intervention is warranted, fix its schedule,
matched charges and causal interpretation separately before any new worlds.
Closed reserves and all original qualification requirements remain unchanged.
