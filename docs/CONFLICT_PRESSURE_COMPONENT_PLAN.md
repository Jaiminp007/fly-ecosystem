# Conflicting-cue pressure components

On the already used adjacent-opposed development histories, evaluate saved
A/B/C/D taste weights on the fixed A/B cue codebooks using evaluator-only
valences. Report per-history and per-D-survivor taste separation and log loss;
do not expose labels to learning. This is an outcome-selected descriptive
census, not a physical outcome or confidence bound.

Separately perform a fixed 2-by-2 A/B saved-taste and saved-steering crossover
on the observe arm's own D bodies, in A and B probe environments. Verify A/B
motor weights match and keep them fixed; pay identical 11 taste plus 288
steering copy operations, preserve inherited debt and match the physical
starting worlds. Measure taste, steering and interaction in physical energy
and net food, with full captured-source replay and independent setup/ledger
checks. No probe result or counterfactual is used as a training label.

The eight histories and cue curriculum are outcome-informed; none of these
diagnostics replaces the original benchmark, establishes functional pressure
or opens a reserve. Any subsequent learner revision must be specified before
new outcomes and must still pass all unchanged original qualification gates.
