Status: the scorer and independent inference checker are implemented on invented
engineering tables. **No performance protocol or fresh seed bank is frozen or
registered.** The complete collector, source/exclusion checks, budget enforcement
and independent full performance-stream audit must pass preflight first.

This is one human-proposed fixed mechanism/representation/replay/contact bundle,

## Required joint tests

`learning/value-development.mjs` defines **26** comparisons, all required:

- Fourteen continuing-sequence comparisons: candidate over original-observation
  value, contact-observation actor, original-observation actor, lambda=0, frozen,
  reset, blind, shuffled, incumbent, four fixed motor policies and random.
- B and C acquisition: B:B over B:A, and C:C over C:B on common end-C bodies.
- Separate acquired A and B advantages over their zero-weight reset policies.
- Separate final A and B advantages retaining at least 95% of their initial
  acquired advantage: `V(f,C)-.95*V(f,f)-.05*V(f,reset)`.
- Original-ecology transfer: end-C over reset, accept-cruise and accept-stationary.
- Functional pressure: A:A minus A:B exceeds 5% of the acquired A advantage;
  also require positive A:A minus A:B beneficial intake less toxin damage.
- Null cue equivalence inside +/-0.5 energy per original founder.

The functional pressure assay reinstalls genuinely trained A/B weights into the
same end-C bodies with the same RNG, debt and food inventory. All learned probe
versions have identical inference compaction and retain only the selected weights
as acquired inference state. The A:A copy/reinstall sham must replay exactly.
Shared parameters, replay overflow or a changed readout alone do not pass pressure.
Positive acquired A/B skills, useful B acquisition and final retention are required
jointly with the transient interference and physical-intake comparisons.

Twenty-three survival guards require nonnegative summed paired survivor-count
differences across histories, using the original 24 founders. These cover every
sequence and acquired-skill/acquisition/retention/transfer comparison. Exact ties
are permitted and do not establish a survival benefit. Each history must have
at least twelve founders with actual updates and changed weights. A new explicit
support guard also requires a surviving candidate at the final sequence endpoint,
an end-C checkpoint, and nonempty bodies in every learned/reset frozen probe.
An empty population cannot supply acquired skill through differences in debt.

## Inference and budget definitions

Use eight development histories and a separate sixteen-history confirmation
reserve. Development requires every directional mean positive, null mean inside
its margin, all exact-count guards, activity and nonempty support. It is a screen,
not qualification. Confirmation requires every directional Student-t lower bound
positive and exact one-sided sign-flip p <= .05/26. Null equivalence requires its
two-sided interval wholly inside +/-0.5 using the stricter .05/52 quantile.
History-level independence and symmetric paired effects for sign-flips remain
assumptions; neither software tests nor the t model prove them.

The fixed family has grown from 22 to 26 because the observation/mechanism and
lambda controls are now mandatory. No outcome-driven test removal is allowed.
The independent SciPy checker reconstructs quantiles, all effects, uncertainty,
enumerated sign-flips, exact-count guards, activity, support and final flags.
Reset initialization has zero abstract arithmetic under the declared zero-array
allocation convention. Every recorded reset construction charge must be zero;
no apparent skill may come from an initialization penalty.

Per history: 17 continuing worlds of at most 1,792 ticks plus 15 unique frozen
probes and one identical sham of at most 256 ticks. Thus 33 worlds / 34,560
collection world-steps; 24 histories total 792 worlds / 829,440 world-steps.
Fresh exclusions must cover all prior protocols/reserves and named engineering
sources before seeds are generated. Every captured source is bound before any
performance observation; a failed development bank closes its reserve.

Proposed ceilings in `VALUE_BUDGET`: 400 billion actual policy scalar operations;
512 MiB compressed and 8 GiB uncompressed tape per history; 16 GiB total evidence;
24 hours each collection/replay CPU and wall; 48 hours independent-audit CPU and
wall. They account for the new value/history/replay work and four added arms,
not a reduction in per-body energy prices. Actual CPU/wall/storage and replay/
audit work are reported separately. Initialization must check available space.
Ceiling failure preserves partial evidence and closes the bank without success.
