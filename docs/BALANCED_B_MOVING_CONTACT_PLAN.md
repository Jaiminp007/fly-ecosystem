# Balanced B-phase moving-contact opportunities

For each recorded D survivor in both reused development arms, inspect all
B-phase decisions with odor-present homogeneous contact, regardless of motor
action. Count contacts, accepted contacts, accepted contacts while moving at
half or full speed, and the actually observed own nonzero food signals after
those accepted moving actions. Separate survivors with and without B examples;
verify the original funnel, trace hashes and own decision/observation pairing.

Pre-movement contact is not proof of the result of stopping: resources,
competition and random trajectories can differ. Actual moving outcomes are
not counterfactual stopped outcomes. This survivor-selected descriptive census
cannot qualify a learner, infer unobserved labels or open a reserve.
