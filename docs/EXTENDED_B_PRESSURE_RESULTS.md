# Extended-B development: extinction and missing probes

The fixed 512/1024/512/256 schedule gave the existing B task twice as much
continuous exposure, without refilling bodies or food. This was a harder,
outcome-informed development diagnostic on the same eight histories, not a
replacement for the original schedule or a fresh qualification.

- Observe retained 73/192 founders at the end of B; all eight worlds became
  extinct during C, so none produced a D checkpoint or pressure/transfer probe.
- Filter retained 71/192 at the end of B; six worlds became extinct during C.
  The other two reached D with one and two survivors, respectively.
- The required eight-history retention, interference and transfer comparisons
  are incomplete and fail the unchanged gate. Missing probes are not imputed.

This result shows that the proposed schedule is not viable for these learners
and histories; it does not isolate B exposure as the sole cause of death, since
age, food and all later trajectories also change. It does not demonstrate
useful B skill, functional interference, recovery or original-ecology transfer.
Do not tune the duration on this evidence or open a closed reserve.

Eight focused tests pass. Compressed tapes and their uncompressed event hashes,
protocol, sources, checkpoints and results are in
`evidence/extended-b-development/20261026-v1`. The main readback checks 566,965
decision/receipt pairs, 86 checkpoint links and 26 available probe starts. The
acquisition readback checks 559,295 decisions, 335,469 scores, 28,563 vetoes,
masked cues, pending actions, encoded state and 50 exact source replays. The
outcome checker verifies available comparisons and 1,180 A-probe receipts.
None independently simulates physics or measures hardware energy. The original
benchmark, prior failures, reserves and full staged objective remain unchanged.
