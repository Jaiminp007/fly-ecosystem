# Supported learner pressure factorial

The fixed `SUPPORTED_PRESSURE_FACTORIAL_PLAN.md` study crossed A/B taste and
steering models on each reused protect D world for both A and B probe seeds.
All arms preserved matched motor models and debt and paid 326 component-copy
operations. Each contrast is B minus A for the named component, measured as
budget-adjusted energy per founder.

| Probe | Taste at A steering | Taste at B steering | Steering at A taste | Steering at B taste | Interaction |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A | +0.010310 | +0.010365 | +0.007658 | +0.007713 | +0.000054 |
| B | -0.006909 | +0.035527 | -0.001419 | +0.041018 | +0.042436 |

On average the A-probe B taste and B steering replacements both increase
energy rather than produce the required earlier-task pressure. Their net
intake changes at A steering/taste are +0.009459 and +0.009366 per founder,
respectively. B-probe effects depend on the other component: the combined
interaction is positive, but the individual replacements at A settings are
slightly negative. No survivor differences occur in any factorial contrast.
Several history-level differences are very small; strict positive-history
counts in the archived results are signs, not statistical evidence.

These are total-world interventions on reused, outcome-selected development
worlds. Changed trajectories can alter interactions and RNG; they do not prove
individual component effects or fresh qualification. The study does not compare
against cheap transfer controls, and the original joint pressure failure and
all reserve boundaries remain unchanged.

Evidence: `evidence/supported-pressure-factorial/20261026-v1`. The independent
checker reconstructs 480 charged component assignments, verifies 115,532
decision/receipt pairs, frozen models and debt, and exactly replays 64 arms.
Two focused factorial tests pass. It does not independently simulate physics
or prove low-level hardware-energy adequacy.
