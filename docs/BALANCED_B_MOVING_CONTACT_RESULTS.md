# Balanced B-phase moving-contact census

The predeclared census examines the B acquisition records of D survivors in
the two reused development arms. Contact means recorded own odor-present,
homogeneous local contact before movement; food signals are the actual own
outcomes after the recorded action, not outcomes under a hypothetical stop.

| Arm / B examples | Survivors | Accepted moving-contact events | Survivors with such events | Survivors with positive / negative moving food |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Rbase / some | 34 | 1,050 | 21 | 18 / 15 |
| Rbase / none | 26 | 1,230 | 14 | 12 / 10 |
| Blend / some | 27 | 620 | 18 | 15 / 11 |
| Blend / none | 28 | 1,253 | 15 | 12 / 14 |

For zero-example survivors, 14/26 rbase and 15/28 blend had at least one
accepted moving homogeneous contact. The other 12/26 and 13/28 did not; a
stopping intervention alone cannot be assumed to address their observed access
gap. Positive and negative categories overlap: they count survivors having at
least one respective signal, not exclusive outcomes or inferred cue labels.

Evidence is in `evidence/balanced-b-moving-contact/20261026-v1`. An independent
checker reconstructs the survivor sets and B example counts from saved states,
verifies hashes, and checks 58,880 decision/observation pairs and all reported
counts. It does not independently simulate physics. Selection on D survival,
shared worlds and reused development histories prevent causal or qualification
claims. Neither these opportunities nor moving outcomes establish what would
happen if an agent stopped.

The next diagnostic is one fixed matched stopping witness: on an accepted,
odor-present homogeneous contact, execute the stationary action instead of a
moving action, with an otherwise identical shadow control and equal sensor,
selection, storage and work charges. Preserve executed-action credit and own
factual outcomes. Predeclare its stage boundaries and comparisons before any
run; measure acquired-example access, physical outcomes and original pressure
and transfer gates. This compiled witness is only a headroom intervention,
not an acquired skill, an independent qualification, or autonomous revision.
