# Frozen taste/steering crossover

This post-outcome factorial intervention isolates saved A/B taste and
cue-shared steering models on the eight reused filter D worlds. A and B motor
weights matched for all 58 D survivors. Each arm starts from the same physical
world within its probe, receives the same paid sensor, and pays the same 299
additional component-copy operations. No new learning or hidden valence is used.

| B minus A component | A-probe mean energy / founder | B-probe mean energy / founder |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Taste, holding steering at A | +0.003512 | +0.001452 |
| Steering, holding taste at A | +0.067518 | -0.110712 |
| Taste–steering interaction | +0.012794 | +0.039290 |
| Both B versus both A | +0.083825 | -0.069971 |

The observed full-model pressure contrasts are mostly associated with changing
steering in these interventions, while interaction and world dynamics also
matter. In A, history `2672461838` contributes +0.528650 to the steering-at-A
contrast; in B, `1917475589` contributes -0.837500. These outcome-selected
histories are not independent confirmation or evidence that a particular
founder's steering caused an isolated outcome: competition, future observations
and RNG can change across total-world interventions. This does not establish
functional pressure, retained acquired skills, successful transfer, fresh
qualification or autonomous improvement.

Captured inputs, source hashes, checkpoints, tapes and results are in
`evidence/taste-steering-crossover/20261026-v1`. Three focused tests pass. The
independent readback verifies 464 component assignments, equal setup billing,
116,206 decision/receipt pairs, outcome arithmetic and 64 exact captured-source
replays. It does not independently simulate physics or prove hardware energy
costs. Existing failures and closed reserves remain unchanged.
