## Candidate: forced-first-fire (blind adopt @512, evaluated @1024)

Motivation: C13's G2b (+0.030) confounds first-fire SELECTION (who
adopts at 512) with second-fire REVISION (what re-firing adds).
Force adoption at 512 (blind, C7-pattern, no sandbox/fee), then
evaluate at 1024 — isolates pure revision value (A = forced +
evaluated vs B = forced + kept; G2b = A−B).

## Why it is KILLED (three independent collapses)

1. **No clean isolation with the menu-closed set.** The evaluated
   second fire needs a real choice to have revision value. With a
   single-op menu it can only re-affirm (C13 already measured that:
   +0.030). With a multi-op menu it reintroduces selection-at-step-2
   (C8's problem: sandbox optimizes train error). Forcing step 1
   moves selection; it does not remove it.
2. **No drop op exists.** The informative second-step action —
   abandoning the forced adoption on negative evidence — is not in
   the append-only menu (indices 0–7 are all adoptions). A keep/drop
   evaluation cannot be built without a new operator, and no new
   operator is known (open list stands).
3. **The predictable version re-confirms C11.** A {6,2} second-step
   choice would sandbox-pick gain-2.0 (MENU SWEEP: index 2 always
   wins evaluations) and C11 already measured gain-2.0 live transfer
   as null. Spending 20 fresh mains to re-confirm C11 is shopping,
   not discovery.

Verdict: forced-first-fire is C13-with-forced-start, not a new
causal question. NOT licensed. Killed before scoping (no scoping
doc will be written for it).
