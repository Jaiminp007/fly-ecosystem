# Supported pressure paired-prefix diagnosis

For every reused protect D world, compare existing saved-A and saved-B probe
traces separately on A and B probe seeds. Verify their saved physical worlds
match. Read both complete traces and verify their stored hashes and lengths;
compare event streams only up to the first change in an executed controller
output, charged extra energy, or physical/observation receipt. Record the
tick, body, output categories, and matching prefix length. A matching prefix
establishes only equality of recorded earlier events, not a claim about later
causal contributions or statistical qualification.

This small read-only diagnosis preserves all prior evidence and uses no new
physical rollout or reserve. The physically paid factorial remains necessary
to distinguish taste and steering effects and still requires disk space.
