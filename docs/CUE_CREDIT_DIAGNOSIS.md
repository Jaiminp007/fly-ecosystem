# Cue / outcome credit engineering diagnosis

On 2026-10-24, continuation resumed directly at the user's request. This is a
deterministic source-boundary investigation, not another performance study or a
replacement learner. Previous failed banks, confirmation reserves, captured

## Reproduction

Run `node --test tests/cue-credit.test.mjs` or
`node scripts/audit-cue-credit.mjs --output PATH`. The CLI creates its output
exclusively and refuses overwrite. The local generated record is
`evidence/cue-credit/20261024-v1/record.json`. It fingerprints the engine, cue
wrapper, mushroom learner and diagnostic entrypoint; it is not a complete
portable source freeze or an independently reconstructed physical audit.

Three fixed synthetic two-founder worlds each advance one physical tick with
learning, reproduction, resource regrowth and mutation disabled. Only the focal
body eats. Bodies start at energy 50, with no obstacles; disclosed fixture speed
is 30. Experimental outputs are forced, not selected from performance outcomes.
Every fixture is replayed from its initial physical checkpoint and compared
against both its complete resulting checkpoint and recorded observations/ledger.
All physical accounting residuals are checked. These artificial body/resource
placements do not establish learner-reachable states or prevalence in an ecology.

## Reproduced boundaries

- **Contact versus ranked target:** the observed cue is patch 1, a small positive
  patch two units away, while raw directional sensors point toward patch 2, a
  larger harmful patch thirty units away. Stationary intake is positive 0.1.
- **Mixed-contact intake:** patch 1 is nearest and positive, but both a positive
  and a harmful patch are consumed in the same tick. Positive intake is about
  0.1 and toxin damage 0.8; the next previous-food signal is about -0.7 despite
  the sensed positive cue. It is an aggregate, not a labeled individual taste.
- **Movement crosses contact:** before movement, only the positive patch 1 is
  within the 15-unit contact radius. After the forced movement, that patch is
  outside the radius and harmful patch 2 is inside; only patch 2 is consumed,
  producing toxin damage 0.8. A pre-movement contact cue need not label the
  outcome of that tick. Native heading noise remains enabled and checkpointed.

The engine samples sensors before movement and evaluates consumption afterward,
over every patch in contact, not just the nearest cue or ranked direction target.
The diagnostic independently calculates the ranked target for these untied
fixtures only; this does not establish general sensor provenance verification.

Two additional **synthetic learner API schedules**, separate from those worlds,
exercise the existing mushroom learner. A decision at tick 1 receives one cue;
own energy falls from 50 to 49 at tick 4. `observe` receives own energy each tick,
but no intervening cue observations. Either a second decision at tick 9 receives
another cue, or a terminal observation at tick 8 closes the interval. Both emit
one duration-eight transition with reward -1/120 and the tick-1 pending features.
The terminal schedule tests API arithmetic, not a physical death trajectory.

The production cue wrapper also supplies observations only at decisions every
eight ticks. Mushroom updates use own net energy change, including costs and
budget debt, rather than isolated food valence. This schedule can credit costs
and later encounters to earlier features. That is not inherently an invalid
policy-gradient interval, and it does not prove the cause of the failed bank.
No incidence or performance estimate was derived from these constructed cases.

## Consequences for the next candidate

An identity-preserving odor code fixes representational ambiguity, not this
cue/outcome association boundary. Simply pairing the current cue with previous
food is also insufficient: the signal refers to the preceding step and can mix
opposite patch outcomes. Do not silently treat it as a privileged valence label.

Before new performance seeds, specify a single prospective learner with explicit
observation timing, credit interval, permitted own sensory/taste history and
neutral behavior. Any faster sampling, motor change or retained history needs
its own operation/storage bill and matched controls. Observer instrumentation
may record patch identities for diagnosis but cannot give them to the policy.
Keep own net-energy evaluation, all-founder death/debt accounting, separate A/B
retention, genuine pressure checks, original-ecology transfer against reset and
same-interface always-accept, and unchanged survival gates. Changing observation
cadence is an intervention, not a free correction to the old frozen learner.
