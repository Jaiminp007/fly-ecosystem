# Decoupled pressure component diagnosis

The predeclared factorial in `DECOUPLED_PRESSURE_COMPONENT_PLAN.md` reuses
eight candidate D worlds and their A/B probe seeds. This outcome-informed
diagnostic is not qualification. Each frozen arm copies the A or B taste model
and independently the A or B steering model, pays 326 scalar copy operations,
and preserves the same world and unpaid debt. A and B motor weights match for
all D survivors. Effects are B minus A for the named component.

| Probe and metric | Taste at A steering | Taste at B steering | Steering at A taste | Steering at B taste | Interaction |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A energy/founder | -0.000577 | +0.001620 | -0.085846 | -0.083649 | +0.002197 |
| A net intake/founder | -0.002319 | -0.027085 | +0.005249 | -0.019517 | -0.024766 |
| B energy/founder | +0.029433 | -0.041615 | +0.068203 | -0.002845 | -0.071048 |
| B net intake/founder | +0.018643 | -0.068749 | +0.069599 | -0.017792 | -0.087392 |

The mean A-energy pressure is principally associated with the steering
swap in this particular setup, not the taste swap. The net-intake contrasts
do not establish a consistent steering-driven A intake loss: their signs
depend on the taste setting. B results show interaction and heterogeneity,
so these means cannot establish a standalone learned component or justify a
new qualification claim. These are total-world interventions, including
competition and RNG effects, not isolated individual effects. The additional
copy cost also makes these outcomes distinct from earlier probe outcomes.

Evidence: `evidence/decoupled-pressure-crossover/20261026-v1`. The independent
checker reconstructs 496 component assignments and their setup billing,
checks 123,639 decisions and receipts and frozen weights, and exactly replays
64 arms using captured source. It does not independently simulate physics or
prove the physical adequacy of the work/energy model. Five focused crossover
and physical-model tests pass. The original transfer failure, pressure gates,
closed reserves and fresh-qualification requirements remain unchanged.

The next hypothesis is that the candidate's A/B steering history contributes
to its inconsistent transfer, rather than treating the taste residual as the
sole cause. Test a predeclared, paid A/B steering crossover on the same
candidate D worlds and original transfer probes, with matched taste and motor
models, before considering any revision; do not use the diagnostic as fresh
evidence or compare unpaired candidate and control worlds as if paired.
