## Convergent findings (all pair-stable unless noted)
1. **Intake edge is law-like**: mut/copy out-eat naive on 8/8
   bank-pairs across shared colonies, heir colonies, birth probes,
   windows, and both noise doses. Never reversed.
2. **Deaths are always energy (starvation), never age**: the colony
   ecology is boom-bust; costs are flat across arms, so the edge is
   front-loaded intake, not efficiency.
3. **Born<450 all die** (0.00, 90+ offspring, 3 banks, all arms):
   colonies are two-phase (death zone + censored tail); pooled survival
   mixes selection with censoring.
4. **Fate benefit is bank-scale-conditional**: 1792-colony copy−naive
   gaps split pairs in every line; the 900-tick window stabilizes V1
   (4/4 clear) but not V2 (ceiling compresses pair 2, mut reverses).
   Pooled ordering copy > mut > naive holds on both window banks.
5. **Transmission is task-bound and practice-fragile**: nothing crosses
   to novel tasks (BIRTHCARRY flat; CUE relabels kill); same-world
   practice dissolves edges (ERODE 0.11, HOME 0.25, MUTHOME2 −0.02).
6. **Dose matters in level, not kind**: 0.01 narrows the fate deficit
   (pooled +0.126 vs +0.012) without stabilizing it; intake holds at
   both doses; birth is free at both doses.

## Mechanism addendum (2026-11-04, zero-world cut of SHORTCOL/SHORTCOL3 rows)
Energy curves pin ALL arms at the same ceiling early (max bin-mean
45.7–45.8 on every colony, both banks — placement is 48, i.e.
everyone starts capped) and decline together to ~21–25 by tick 900.
Verdict: BOTH mechanisms operate — early intake is wasted at the cap
(cap waste) and late survival is shared starvation (depletion
timing). Intake cannot convert because there is no regime in the
colony where marginal food changes anyone's fate: full at the start,
starving together at the end, declining together in the middle. This
closes the mechanism question with existing evidence; no new colony
can separate these without retuning the world (banned). Any future
survival contrast must change the regime (e.g. capped placement
energy — a new manipulation, not a kinder world).
- Social substrate (sensors/actuators for need-conditioned transfer)
  remains the ACT-void shopping list; population culture needs it.
