# Taste-contact provenance: overlap-associated ambiguity

On the 56 outcome-selected D survivors, the replay found 15,678 A/B/C factual
stationary-accept examples. All 1,508 cases where the selected cue's patch
valence disagreed with the body's net intake sign had multiple contacts and
at least one opposite-valence contact at the pre-action position. In 1,434 of
those cases the physical receipt contained both beneficial intake and toxin
damage; the other 74 had one-sided measured intake. The selected patch had
positive pre-action amount in every example, but competing bodies can change
amounts before feeding. This association is not a per-patch consumption trace
or a causal intervention.

An outcome-informed, hypothetical filter retaining only examples where all
pre-action contact cues match the selected cue retains 11,355/15,678 examples
and has zero observed cue-valence disagreements. Single-contact filtering
retains 9,488, also with zero observed disagreements. These are repeated,
selected trajectories from eight reused histories, not independent error-rate
estimates or proof of a safe general rule. In particular, cue equality is not
in itself a universal guarantee of equal valence; the simulator's fixed
codebook is relevant. A real implementation must make the additional cue
observation explicit, meter comparisons, storage and all controls, preserve
factual labels and test physical learning rather than reuse evaluator valence.

All 1,788 retained D-memory records match their own earlier tick and factual
target, including the previous 191 disagreements. Twenty-four captured-source
replays reproduce original event hashes and final checkpoints. The independent
readback checks 798,663 original events and links all 15,678 decisions,
receipts and observations. Contact snapshots rely on instrumented replay, not
an independently implemented physical simulator. Results, records, source
hashes and the separate cue census are in
`evidence/taste-contact-provenance/20261026-v1`.

This is a diagnostic, not an acquired skill, physical benefit, fresh
qualification or autonomous improvement. Existing failures and closed
reserves remain unchanged.
