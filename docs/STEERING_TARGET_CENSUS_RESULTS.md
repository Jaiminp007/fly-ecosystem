# Steering targets and learned taste

The post-outcome AA versus AB prefix census holds taste at its saved A model
and changes only saved steering. In all 16 A/B probe pairs, the first observed
divergence is a steering command, not a preceding own-observation, recorded
controller-cost or physical-receipt difference. Prefix equality ends at that
command; it does not isolate later outcomes or prove a focal causal mechanism.

The separately instrumented AA/AB replays classify the recommended pairs
using evaluator-only cue valence. For AA, 8,567/9,836 available A-probe pairs
and 6,985/9,190 available B-probe pairs include at least one harmful patch.
At least one target cue differs from the body's current cue in 8,363 A and
8,389 B available decisions. These are repeated decisions on selected bodies,
not independent samples or evidence of a harmful outcome for each pair.

On the same AA target observations, evaluating the saved taste models gives:

| Probe | Taste model | Mean acceptance, beneficial targets | Mean acceptance, harmful targets |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| A | A | 0.849 | 0.247 |
| A | B | 0.854 | 0.224 |
| B | A | 0.697 | 0.719 |
| B | B | 0.717 | 0.650 |

The learned A taste model distinguishes the observed A targets, while the B
model shows a smaller separation on the observed B targets. These are offline
predictions, not inputs actually supplied to the steering learner, physical
interventions or proof of a retained skill. The present midpoint selector uses
amount and geometry, not target cue or learned taste value. A candidate worth
testing is to expose target cues explicitly and route factual learned taste
predictions into target evaluation, charging sensing, predictions, memory and
equally equipped controls. That intervention is not implemented or qualified.

`evidence/steering-prefix-census/20261026-v1` contains hashed source-tape
prefixes; `evidence/steering-target-census/20261026-v1` contains the target and
taste-snapshot results. The independent target readback reconstructs 38,224
available decisions in 32 exact source replays and checks category and model
arithmetic. It does not independently simulate physics or establish the cost
or benefit of a new sensor. All prior failures and closed reserves remain.
