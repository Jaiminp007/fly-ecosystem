# Factual A-probe prediction comparison

The predeclared `CONTACT_A_FACTUAL_PREDICTION_PLAN.md` evaluates saved A and
B taste models on the same cues in recorded AA probe trajectories. Events
are stationary, accepted, odor-present homogeneous contacts with a nonzero
actual own food signal. The reported B prediction is an offline calculation;
there is no observed B-model outcome on the AA trajectory.

On these recorded stop-world events, saved B predictions move in opposite
directions for positive and negative own signals on average. This is consistent
with improved discrimination in this selected sample, **not** a measurement of
BA physical benefit or a causal attribution to A-history replay. Selection on
AA acceptance and D survival, repeated events per body and shared worlds limit
interpretation. The raw A pressure gate remains failed.
