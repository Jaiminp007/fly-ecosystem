# Focal controller crossover

For the two previously lost, outcome-selected founders, switching only the
focal body to the cheap native-full program rescues it in both other-body
backgrounds. Retaining its paid residual learner leaves it dead in both:

| History / founder | All residual | Focal cheap, others residual | Focal residual, others cheap | All cheap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1172370180 / 2 | dead | alive (9.742 energy) | dead | alive (7.571) |
| 1917475589 / 2 | dead | alive (25.524 energy) | dead | alive (19.690) |

All arms paid the same mask and byte-scan setup before the switch and retained
existing debt. The cheap program pays its own native-full step/state cost; it
is not the learned controller running for free. This is a total-world program
intervention, not a fresh qualification, independent sample or training label.

In each background, the focal body's observed commands and intake match exactly
through the paid learner's death: 185 ticks in the first case, and 83/82 ticks
in the second. At those deaths the focal-cheap body's energy exceeds the paid
learner's by 0.473 and 0.192 units respectively; the cumulative recorded total
cost differences are the same. This supports controller-cost pressure along
these particular matched prefixes under the declared accounting. It does not
establish general safety, an independent physical simulation, or what would
happen under unobserved trajectories after death. Other bodies, future food
competition and RNG can diverge after the loss.

The captured protocol, original checkpoints, starts, traces and endpoints are
in `evidence/cue-focal-controller/20261026-v1`. The independent setup/ledger
readback checks 18,699 decisions and receipts, 76 per-body setup assignments and
eight captured-source replays; a separate prefix readback checks the commands,
intake and cost comparisons. The one-operation-per-scanned-byte setup and all
other operation prices remain abstract, not machine-energy measurements.
Existing transfer/survival failures, fresh qualification and RSI remain unmet;
no prior reserve is opened.
