# DISSONANCE AUDIT V1: expensive verdicts are the most hedged — no effort-justification detected

Analytic-only. Zero new worlds, zero new seeds. Executes the R151-specified
dissonance self-audit on 33 published verdicts.
Script: `scripts/dissonance-audit.py` (table + ranks; word counts re-checked inline).

## Method (proxy, transparent, limited)

Hedge-hits per RESULTS doc via fixed lexicon (descriptive/unreplicated/thin/
disclosed/post-hoc/caution/void/weak/luck/noise/hypothesis/... — full list in
script). Crude: counts technical uses too ("noise", "suggest"); word counts
narrow-band (259–790, mostly 350–550) while hedges vary 19× (1–19), so length
explains little (words↔hedges ρ=+0.25). Margin coding absent — noted as the
audit's own limitation (vote-tally pre-registration, JURY prescription, is the
severity upgrade).

## Result: dissonance prediction REJECTED

- Spearman(cost mains, hedges) = **+0.37 raw / +0.28 per-1k-words**
  (dissonance predicts NEGATIVE). Within GO +0.52, within NOGO +0.49 —
  expensive verdicts carry MORE qualification, in both directions.
- Most expensive verdicts: LEAVE V2 (30 mains/14 hedges), CUE (24/11),
  GAIN-DOSE (21/11), ENCOUNTER-V1C (18/15) — the costliest language is
  the most hedged. Least hedged: clean/strong results at low cost
  (ENERGY GO 8/2, FOOD GO 12/2, RSI V1c 12/2) plus one strong negative
  (RSI V1b −0.46 with 160:1 mechanism, 12/1).
- **One watch-item (not a finding):** RSI V1d (32 mains, 5 hedges —
  below median) closes the whole selection line strongly. Case for
  honesty: bit-identical copied==fixed + 47/48 stampede mechanism +
  pooled pattern = thick evidence, hedges track strength here too. Watch
  means: if V1d's closure is ever revisited, re-audit first.

## Verdict (methodological — no GO/NOGO)

- **NO effort-justification detected:** hedging tracks evidence thinness
  (voids, floors, single-seed concentrations, below-band means, published
  corrections — VALUE-PROBE's 19 hedges on 9 mains is the exemplar: the
  cheapest uncertain verdict is the most qualified).
- The program's confidence language is calibrated to evidence, not cost —
  as far as a lexicon proxy can tell. Limitation stands (margin coding
  absent); the JURY vote-tally prescription remains the upgrade path.
- No bank, instrument, or verdict rescored. Cumulative worlds unchanged.
