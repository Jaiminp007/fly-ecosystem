# Conflicting-cue taste and steering components

On the adjacent-opposed study's outcome-selected D survivors, the saved B
taste model improves balanced B-cue separation relative to saved A but remains
negative: observe changes from -0.1742 to -0.0672, filter from -0.1734 to
-0.0471. Positive separation means greater acceptance of beneficial than
harmful evaluator-labelled cues. The saved B model retains strong descriptive
A-cue separation (0.5839 observe, 0.5798 filter). These are fixed-cue offline
scores, not physical outcomes, confidence bounds or training labels.

At B, observe survivors' retained taste reservoirs contain 1,257 A and 617 B
records; filter has 1,408 A and 701 B. These are repeated samples, not counts
of actual replay updates. Survivor-specific exposure differs substantially:
the naive sum of each founder's proportional A share is about 1,238 observe
and 1,429 filter, close to the observed counts. Thus the aggregate imbalance
does not by itself demonstrate a broken reservoir or causal interference.
The saved reservoir targets agree with evaluator cue valence in this particular
homogeneous-contact sample; this is not a universal guarantee.

The paid 2-by-2 physical crossover on observe D bodies gives, for B minus A:

| Component | A-probe mean energy / founder | B-probe mean energy / founder |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Taste, holding steering at A | -0.005412 | +0.013568 |
| Steering, holding taste at A | -0.041065 | -0.071877 |
| Interaction | -0.005364 | -0.026795 |
| Both B versus both A | -0.051840 | -0.085104 |

The whole-world physical effects are mixed and include trajectory and
competition changes; they do not isolate a particular update's cause. They
reinforce that poor B performance is not solely a taste-score issue. No
functional-pressure or qualification criterion is met. A separately specified
paid recency-replay hypothesis is in `docs/RECENCY_REPLAY_HYPOTHESIS.md`.

`evidence/conflict-taste-snapshots/20261026-v1` contains the checkpoint and
reservoir censuses; independent readbacks check 4,128 cue evaluations and
15,839 retained memory records. `evidence/conflict-pressure-crossover/20261026-v1`
contains the physical study; three focused tests pass and its readback checks
488 assignments, 114,356 decisions/receipts and 64 exact source replays. These
audits do not independently simulate physics or prove hardware energy costs.
Prior failures, original qualification and closed reserves remain intact.
