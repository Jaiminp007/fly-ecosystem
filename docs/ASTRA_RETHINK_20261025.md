## Research boundary

[Niv et al.](https://www.princeton.edu/~ndaw/ndjd07.pdf) motivate the opportunity
cost of time using average reward, while [Wan et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.13855)
extend options to continuing average-reward interaction. Those results do not
establish convergence or performance for this simulator. Astra pointed out that
literal infinite-time average reward degenerates to zero here because lifespans
are finite and death is absorbing without renewal. Opportunity-cost reasoning
can inform a finite-window option model, but cannot silently replace energy,
terminal loss and survival objectives. No biological fidelity is established.

`scripts/check-taste-development.py` checks all nine new attempts' source hashes,
endpoint arithmetic, common probe starts, sham equality, control contrasts,
retention and raw interference arithmetic in under one second. It is explicitly
a brief result readback, not a full trajectory audit or independent learner
reconstruction. Eighteen targeted learner/integration checks pass in 0.627 seconds.
Earlier locked failures and all 96 closed confirmation histories remain unchanged.
Fresh qualification, actual functional-pressure success and every autonomous
revision/search-productivity stage are still outstanding. No RSI is claimed.
