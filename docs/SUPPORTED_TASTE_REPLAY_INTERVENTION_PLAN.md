# Matched taste replay intervention

Test one outcome-informed causal hypothesis: the factual reservoir replay that
revisits A during B contributes to the absence of functional pressure. Compare
two physically acquired supported-steering learners over the original eight
development histories and 512/512/512/256 schedule. Both retain and update
the same reservoir/recent buffers, perform the same four RNG draws, calculate
the same sampled record, run two SGD updates and pay identical declared work
and storage. `mixed` uses the reservoir sample for the second update; `fresh`
uses the current factual record. Their only intended difference is the second
update's factual sample, never a phase label, hidden valence or imagined reward.

Use identical sensing and supported steering, freeze motors after A, and retain
all original saved/current/reset A/B probes and transfer reset/native/guarded/
cheap-native controls. Record full compressed tapes and checkpoints, hashes,
and source closure. Independently reconstruct actual own-contact credit,
reservoir/RNG, both update policies, storage, terminal obligations and replay.

This intervention is a mechanism test, not permission to manufacture forgetting
or relax the original >5% functional-pressure, genuine B acquisition, A net
intake, >=95% A/B retention, transfer, survival and fresh-audit criteria. Raw
reused-data contrasts are exploratory only; no reserve opens. Fail or preserve
all missing or interrupted outcomes rather than claiming success.
