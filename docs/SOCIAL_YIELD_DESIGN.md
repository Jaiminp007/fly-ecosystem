# Own-experience social yield prediction: bounded information test

Prospective exploratory design, October 25, 2026. This is a structurally different
information hypothesis, not another motor law. Native food attraction ignores
social context. Existing mature opposite-sex body senses and aggregate scent
might predict whether a food opportunity yields intake, but rich patches also
attract neighbors. Neither avoid nor follow is supplied as the answer.

Use only the eight factual pursuit-census tapes. Fit independent per-founder
logistic predictors on actual A/B/C experience, never pooled across founders or
histories. Starting at each sensor/action decision, predict any own beneficial
intake during that transition and the next seven. Admit a training label only
when all eight outcomes have occurred. Do not cross phase boundaries or include
return-A data; report the last seven censored decisions in each observed segment.
The tape contains actual chosen commands, not counterfactual choices.

Freeze every weight before native-full transfer. Evaluate every complete transfer
window, including overlapping windows, with history-level reporting. Models:
food-only; food plus current social senses; same-dimensional social-blind; food
plus own social observations delayed 32 decisions; own learned prevalence only.
The delayed control is not an independent shuffle and may retain information.
Features are bias, raw food bearing/proximity, energy, age, walls, previous intake
and the first three own commands; social fields are raw 6--9 and 12. No target
identity, map, geometry, other's intake, observer flag or future input enters.
Use fixed SGD .025, L2 .001 except bias, weights clipped to +/-8; no tuning.

Report Brier and log loss, prevalence, sample/support counts, censored labels,
all five models and their nominal metered arithmetic. Offline measurement is
not a charged embodied policy or an energy gain. The descriptive information
rule requires social to improve both losses in at least 6/8 histories over each
of food-only, blind and delayed controls, and improve their history-mean losses.
No significance, acquired control or causal competition claim follows a pass.

Report executed-command support explicitly: acquisition used stationary/half
speed, while native-full transfer uses full speed. Failure may reflect this
policy/geometry/valence distribution shift; success would still not establish
causal competition, an effective alternative action or qualification. Do not
respond to either outcome with a feature/rate/horizon sweep on these tapes.
