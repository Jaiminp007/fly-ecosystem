# Cue-shared steering: development gates failed

The fixed outcome-informed comparison completed on eight reused histories.
Cue-shared acquisition retained 56/192 founders at D; the replay comparator
retained 68/192. Both used the same indexed selector and externally scheduled
motor freeze. These are different interacting worlds, not paired individual
effects or fresh qualification.

The unchanged transfer gate **fails** against both required controls. The
same-D-body descriptive retention ratios are 95.017% for A and 104.227% for B,
with positive saved-over-reset mean advantages. The A estimate barely exceeds
95%; neither ratio is a confidence bound, fresh confirmation, or proof of
functional pressure. The pressure contrasts also fail to establish the desired
mechanism: mean A:A minus A:B is -0.086361 per founder, B:B minus B:A is
-0.063124, and the A net-intake/toxin difference is -0.125020. Negative A
interference means the B snapshot performed better in that aggregate contrast.
No acquired A/B skill or retention-under-pressure claim follows.

The replay comparator also fails transfer against cheap native-full: +0.679570
per founder, but only 5/8 positive histories. Its A and B descriptive ratios
are 97.316% and 112.288%. Its raw pressure contrasts are outcome-informed,
not statistical qualification or permission to open a reserve.

Frozen sources, inputs, starts, anchors, traces, model snapshots, endpoints
and results are in `evidence/cue-shared-development/20261026-v1`. The initial
readback checks 960,055 decision/receipt pairs, 128 checkpoint links, 208
captured-source probe starts and reported-work/storage/payment ledgers. A
separate post-outcome checker independently reconstructs 445,028 cue-shared
decisions and 280,746 own TD updates, including 10,781 cross-cue transitions;
another checks comparison arithmetic and 60,627 A-probe physical receipts.
These audits do not independently simulate physics, prove complete low-level
cost adequacy or supply statistical confidence bounds. Closed reserves and all
prior failures remain preserved; the full staged goal is unachieved.
