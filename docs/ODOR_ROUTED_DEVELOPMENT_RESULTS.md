# Odor-routed development results

The fixed eight-history study fails the unchanged transfer consistency gate.
Against matched reset, frozen transfer gains +0.418541 energy per original
founder on average, but wins only 5/8 histories; against cheap native-full it
gains +0.364168, again winning 5/8. Aggregate survivor differences are zero for
both comparisons. Against matched guarded steering the mean difference is
-0.548957, with no positive history. These are outcome-informed development
comparisons, not fresh qualification.

At the end of the four-phase acquisition curriculum, learned, matched native,
matched guarded, frozen-after-A and reset-each-phase arms have respectively
20, 27, 26, 60 and 30 survivors out of 192 original founders. All 20 surviving
learned founders actually sampled all three speeds in both routed contexts at
some point during acquisition. This establishes action exposure for those
survivors, not that every founder learned an effective policy or that the
absent samples occurred only in the fourth phase.

On the same end-D physical bodies, descriptive retention ratios are 22.48% for A
and 179.07% for B, computed from positive saved-over-reset mean advantages.
A saved/current/reset probe survivor totals are 17/16/16; B totals are 16/16/15.
The A ratio fails the existing 95% requirement. These reused-history ratios
are not confidence bounds and do not establish distinct skills under functional
pressure; the B ratio cannot offset the A or transfer failures.

The fixed sources, protocol, per-founder action counts, A/B/C/D checkpoints,
frozen probes, complete traces and results are in
`evidence/odor-routed-development/20261026-v1`. One readback verifies 1,398,710
decision/receipt pairs, all trace hashes, frozen weights and reported-work/
storage/payment ledgers; captured-source replay verifies 24 A-to-B/B-to-C/
C-to-D checkpoint links. A separate summary readback checks endpoints, survivor
counts, action exposure, retention arithmetic and transfer comparisons. These
checks do not independently reconstruct every full-study operation, sensory
input or physical dynamic.

All prior failures and closed reserves remain unchanged. Fresh qualification,
functional-pressure retention, original-ecology transfer, and the later
autonomous revision/search-productivity stages remain unproved.
