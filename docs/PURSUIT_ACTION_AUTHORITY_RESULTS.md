## Survival arithmetic disclosure

The frozen scorer averaged floating differences of survival fractions. Against
legacy fast it recorded `-6.938893903907228e-18` and a failed guard despite tied
total survivors (264 versus 264). Thus the original result flags two of three
survival guards, although the exact count-based means are all nonnegative.
This is numerical cancellation, not a survival deficit.

Original protocol, source, rows, traces, results and audits remain unchanged.
A separately fingerprinted working correction uses integer paired survivor-count
sums and the common founder denominator, not an epsilon or weakened rule. Node
and Python regressions cover both a tied floating-negative fixture and a truly
negative count difference. The [software-only comparison](../evidence/pursuit-authority/20261024-v1/scoring-correction/comparison.json)
preserves both guard views and asserts unchanged energy comparisons and overall
failure. No new worlds were run, no qualification follows that view, and no
unchanged rerun is authorized.

  application independently. Exact physical replay supplies that separate scope.

  all 186 tests pass, including eight altered-trace cases and exact-count checks.
  The controller adapter test passes.

No tuning, sample extension, replacement or motor promotion follows this bank.

but is not a learner and does not prove memory pressure. Before another learner
performance bank, original-ecology accessible action headroom still needs a
defensible prospective design. A learner must beat reset AND always-accept with
the same interface; a weaker legacy comparator cannot substitute. Acquisition,
separate earlier-skill retention, actual interference/capacity pressure, survival,
persistent autonomous revisions and copied-improvement/matched-budget search gains
remain required and unachieved. **No RSI has been demonstrated.**
