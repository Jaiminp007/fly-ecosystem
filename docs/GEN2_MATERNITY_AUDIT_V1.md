# GEN2-MATERNITY AUDIT V1: concentration unmeasurable (ID collision) — depth is broad-and-thin

Analytic-only. Zero new worlds, zero new seeds, zero bank draws.
Reads frozen evidence/mate-*/attempt-*/rows lineageFounders
(336 rows) + founderOffspring key ranges + per-row gen-2 counts.
Fulfills the depth half of the R183-Michels correction with a
split verdict: concentration INVALID, census LICENSED.

## Findings

1. **Cross-row maternal concentration is UNMEASURABLE.**
   Founder IDs restart per row (max key 35 anywhere across
   336 worlds × 24+ founders — global uniqueness impossible).
   Pooled "12 distinct mothers / 224 gen-2s" is collision
   artifact (same numbers recur in unrelated worlds: ID 7
   in 28 rows), not lineage concentration. Any cross-row
   pooling of maternal IDs is INVALID; the naive version of
   this audit was built, caught itself, and is published
   here as the caution (never pool world-local IDs).
2. **Depth is broad-and-thin (valid per-row census).** Gen-2
   per-row histogram: 0:174 / 1:115 / 2:35 / 3:9 / 4:3 —
   48% of rows reach gen-2 at least once (broad), never more
   than 4 per row (thin); gen-3 totals 33 births program-wide
   (~10% of rows, at most a few each). Lines touch gen-2
   widely and compound almost never: depth WITHOUT compounding
   (the sink diagnosis, restated by generation: arrivals
   frequent, establishment ~never).
3. **Shapley proper CLOSED as unresolvable** (no counterfactual
   game: single-run histories admit no coalitional variation;
   plus no global IDs). Descriptive pooled maternity CLOSED
   as invalid (finding 1). What WOULD work (specified, not
   built): globally-unique founder IDs (bank+history+index)
   + the per-birth maternal chains that already exist within
   rows (structure present, ID space absent). Runner change
   for a future line; no new worlds needed to specify it.

## Verdict (measurement — split: INVALID + LICENSED)

- **INVALID:** cross-row gen-2 maternity concentration
  (ID-locality proven; pooled numbers retracted before
  publication — the audit caught its own artifact).
- **LICENSED:** per-row depth census (broad-and-thin:
  162/336 rows reach gen-2, max 4/row, gen-3 33 total).
  Depth exists widely and compounds nowhere — the
  compounding-is-depth thesis (R183-corrected) bounded
  above: depth itself is thin (≤4/row), not just rare.
- No bank, instrument, or verdict rescored. Cumulative worlds unchanged.
