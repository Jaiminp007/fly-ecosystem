# Filtered guarded focal-policy results

Five founders differ in survival between the guarded and matched-native runs:
three are lost and two saved. For each, two outcome-selected interventions
change only that founder's mode while all other bodies share the stated policy.

| History / founder | Guarded | Native | Focal native, guarded others | Focal guarded, native others |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3243849148 / 4 | alive | dead | dead | dead |
| 3243849148 / 18 | dead | alive | alive | alive |
| 1917475589 / 3 | alive | dead | alive | alive |
| 3285880790 / 23 | dead | alive | alive | alive |
| 3170855589 / 22 | dead | alive | alive | alive |

For all three lost founders, changing their mode to native in the guarded world
rescues them. But placing each guarded focal in the native world also leaves it
alive. The factual survival differences therefore do not establish a universal
individual guard defect: interactions, food competition and changed future RNG
consumption can affect the outcomes. The same qualification applies to the two
saved founders. This post-outcome-selected causal analysis is not a qualification
test and its counterfactual outcomes cannot be used as training labels.

The result argues against training a per-body steering predictor using only a
static one-step direct-harm label. A future acquired strategy should use each
body's factual history and explicitly test whether its signals generalize under
changing interactions. This is a research implication, not proof that such a
strategy will work or that any specified observation is sufficient.

The frozen protocol, factual timelines, intervention traces, checkpoints and
results are in `evidence/filtered-focal-diagnosis/20261026-v1`. A separate
checker verifies source/input and trace hashes, mode assignments, reported
survival and 48,157 decision/receipt pairs. It does not independently simulate
physics or reconstruct complete charges. This evidence postdates the spatial
archive. The original paid guard remains failed; no fresh reserve is opened.
