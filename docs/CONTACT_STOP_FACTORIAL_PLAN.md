# Contact-stop pressure component diagnostic

On each saved shadow and stop D world from the completed contact-stop study,
cross the original saved A/B taste and steering models in a 2-by-2 factorial.
Use the original A and B probe seeds and 256-tick frozen evaluations. Verify
that the A and B motor banks are identical for every D survivor; preserve the
D-world support state and accumulated unpaid debt. All four interventions
execute and pay the same 326 declared model-copy operations. The first letter
selects taste and the second steering; compare B-minus-A replacements at each
level and their interaction for energy, net intake and survival.

Capture starts, ends, event tapes, input and source hashes. Independently
reconstruct assignments, verify reported work and debt, trace hashes and exact
replay before interpreting the result. Same-world intervention arms can still
diverge in competition and random trajectories; shadow and stop evolved worlds
are distinct. This is an outcome-informed diagnostic, not new training, fresh
qualification, individual causal effects, or permission to relax any gate or
reopen a reserve. Require 1 GiB free before collection and a 100 MiB floor.
