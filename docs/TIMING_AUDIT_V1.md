Analytic-only. Zero new worlds, zero new seeds, zero bank draws.
Reads frozen evidence/mate-*/attempt-*/rows firstMatingTick
(first mating per seed x arm) across 7 timed lines (33

subsequent matings untimed. Null = never-mated (timed lines only).

## Findings

1. **First matings at ticks 186–746, medians 224–294.** All 33
   timed distributions open in the first sixth of the run
   (1792 ticks); the latest first-mating anywhere is tick 746
   (proximity hubR1, 42% of run); 31/33 medians sit below 300
   (~17%). The window opens at ~200 regardless of arm
   (hub/spread/leave/gain/damage all alike) — encounter
   readiness is architectural (maturity + proximity by tick
   200), not interventional (no arm shifts the opening).
2. **Every timed seed mates at least once.** Null fraction 0.00
   in 32/33 distributions (recombine-spread 0.17: 1/6 never).
   Conditional on the field existing, mating initiation is
   certain — the variance is in COUNTS (births_med 4.0–9.0),
   never in onset. Sterility (left-tail audit: dead arms all-
   zero) is failure to initiate, not failure to repeat.
3. **Dead arms have no timing data — twice over.** act/dense/
   energy (all-zero births) predate the field (schema absence),
   AND never mated (null by definition). Whether their
   non-mating is early-absence or late-absence is unknowable
   from frozen rows — the field postdates them. No backfill
   (would need re-runs: forbidden (spent banks)).
4. **Longevity buys dead ticks.** M-POP V2's 14v4v1 survival
   without conversion, explained: extra survival is LATE
   survival (bar-50 founders live longer), but the mating
   window is EARLY (first matings ≤746; medians ≤300). Ticks
   past ~750 buy foraging without mating — survival that
   cannot convert. Any longevity-based Track B rationale is
   refuted in advance by this audit (buys ticks outside the
   window).
