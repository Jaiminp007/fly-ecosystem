# Steering travel-horizon information: failed fixed gate

The fixed 16-step own-return comparison failed. Geometry beat base in 2/8 C
histories and 3/8 transfer histories, and beat delayed geometry in 3/8 C and
7/8 transfer histories. The fixed requirement was at least 6/8 and a lower
mean per-history MSE against both controls in both evaluation families.
Differences in mean MSE (geometry minus base / delayed) were +2.069e-7 /
-2.211e-8 for C and -1.239e-6 / -2.808e-7 for transfer. A transfer-only win
does not pass the joint rule.

The outcome-informed histories are reused development data, not fresh
qualification. These are factual returns under the body's subsequent actions
and interacting world, not counterfactual action values; the design therefore
does not establish or rule out longer-term geometry-sensitive control.

The post-outcome independent readback reconstructs 189,903 training and
81,617 evaluation returns, including 2,077 terminal-absorbed records and
8,080 tape-boundary censored actions. It checks source/input and record hashes,
label timing, per-founder updates, frozen predictions and statistics. It does
not independently simulate physics or pay an embodied predictor's costs.
Protocol, captured sources, records and readback are in
`evidence/steering-travel-horizon/20261026-v1`. The prior one-step failure and
all other closed reserves remain intact; no acquired-skill, pressure, transfer,
fresh qualification or autonomous-revision gate is satisfied.
