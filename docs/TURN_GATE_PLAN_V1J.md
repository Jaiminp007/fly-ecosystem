## Rationale (new — licenses V1j; artifact-control, not a boost attempt)

The line closed AS DEPLOYED (V1i: 8 GOs, replicated mechanism, attributed
floor). This plan reopens it on grounds the closure could not assess:
whether the deployed retention is REAL or a LOSSLESS-COPY ARTIFACT.
Every transfer in V1b–V1i used exact-copy transplants (bit-identical
weights); same-val stayed 0.94–0.95 (no erosion) and niche-ID hit 1.000.
R92/R101 (tutoring + iterated learning: Kirby bottleneck, lossy pupils,
compositionality from transmission bottlenecks) predict exact copying
OVER-preserves: a lossless channel carries noise as faithfully as signal,
so perfect retention proves nothing about learning. The lossy tutor
(weights × 0.5 at transplant) is the artifact-control the program never
ran: if flipped transfer IMPROVES under loss, the bottleneck structures
(retain R92's lossy-tutoring + R101's bottleneck-creates-structure);
if it merely degrades, exact-copy was load-bearing and retention is real
but fragile. Either outcome re-prices the V1b–V1i evidentiary stock
(fingerprint included) — that re-pricing, not a gain, is the product.
No V1k without new rationale.
