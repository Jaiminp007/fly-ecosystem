# Nonfocal transfer path and RNG census

Use the four pre-existing outcome-selected reservoir focal cases for which both
focal-swap tapes executed no focal midpoint action. Replay the original all-
current and all-native transfer starts with the captured source and compare,
at each tick, the checkpointed physical RNG state, living body ids, focal
position/heading and energy, focal own sensor vector and executed turn, and
total food inventory. Report first divergence and duration, and verify both
full trace hashes and final checkpoints against the original study. No observer
information is supplied to the learner or affects physics or prices.

Identical RNG states and living rosters would rule out a different global RNG
stream during those matched ticks, not prove that competition caused an outcome:
food geometry, sensors and coupled trajectories can differ. Conversely, a
different state or roster reveals coupling but not its causal contribution.
This post-outcome descriptive check is not an intervention, fresh qualification,
or a substitute for original-ecology, retention and pressure gates. Preserve
all historical evidence and do not select a policy based on these cases alone.
