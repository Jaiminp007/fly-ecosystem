# Supported steering declared-work audit

The independent schedule-based check in `SUPPORTED_STEERING_WORK_PLAN.md`
reconciles all 64 acquisition tapes: 563,740 steering decisions, 563,356
nonterminal pending updates and 263 terminal updates. Every recorded steering
work counter and every final work/pending/dead state agrees with the declared
74-operation decision, 1,199-operation pending update and 1,151-operation
terminal update schedule. All captured source and trace hashes match.

Evidence is `evidence/supported-steering-development/20261026-v1/steering-work-readback.json`,
generated by `scripts/check-supported-steering-work.mjs`. This verifies that
the reported counters follow the declared accounting schedule; it does **not**
independently prove that the schedule covers every underlying instruction,
that transient memory is fully priced, or that its abstract energy prices match
physical hardware. The earlier ledger check verifies how reported work and
storage enter the simulated charges.

The supported arm still fails functional pressure, and the paid taste/steering
factorial remains unrun pending at least 1 GiB free disk. No new qualification
claim, reserve opening or physical rollout follows from this accounting audit.
