# Matched-RNG resource attribution

Use only the three already identified reservoir cases whose recorded physical
RNG and living roster match for all 256 transfer ticks. Replay both original
worlds from their captured starts, instrumenting patch-amount writes outside the
learner. Preserve exact original traces and final checkpoints. Attribute each
actual feeding write to the body's existing physical receipt; distinguish
pre-decision environmental regrowth from body consumption.

For each focal body, locate the first tick with a nonzero difference in own
food intake while its pre- and post-movement position and the physical RNG
states remain identical. Record the focal's locally reachable patch identities
and quantities at consumption, and prior same-patch nonfocal consumption in
both worlds. If no such tick exists, or exact replay and attribution fail,
report that outcome rather than choosing a different case or threshold. Do not
expose patch identities, other bodies' consumption, observer labels, or fork
outcomes to the learner. Physical receipt sums, patch changes, RNG states and
trace hashes require an independent readback.

This is a fixed outcome-selected mechanistic observation, not an intervention,
fresh qualification, proof that social sensing can predict the gap, or a
license to change any retention, pressure, transfer or survival gate.
