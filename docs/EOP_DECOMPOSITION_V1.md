# EOP DECOMPOSITION V1: topology (circumstance) explains 64% of lifetime energy, 46% of end-state

Analytic-only. Zero new worlds, zero new seeds, zero bank draws.
Roemer-style decomposition on 120 frozen paired hub-vs-spread rows (60 pairs).
Script: `scripts/eop-decomposition.py`.

## Method (frozen)

Topology assignment is CIRCUMSTANCE (randomized by design — histories are
random draws, arm assigned); within-arm variation is EFFORT + LUCK
(unsplit: no effort measure exists — the residual is named honestly).
Outcomes: lifetime energy (AUC over active ticks) + end energy (last 100
active ticks). Paired same-seed design holds background fixed.

## Findings

- **AUC: circumstance share 0.639.** Hub mean 68,941 vs spread 80,063
  (−13.9% lifetime energy from topology alone). Nearly two-thirds of
  lifetime-energy variance is the patch you were assigned.
- **End-state: circumstance share 0.455.** Hub 29.5 vs spread 38.7
  (−23.8% level, but only 46% of the VARIANCE). Endpoints vary more
  idiosyncratically — late recovery/crashes (GAS) are luck-driven.
- **Reading:** topology sets the TRAJECTORY (64%); luck writes the
  ENDING (54% within-arm at end). The poverty trap is circumstantial in
  origin, idiosyncratic in resolution — consistent with GAS (alarm
  sets it, turnover scrambles the end).

## Verdict (measurement — no GO/NOGO)

- **LICENSED:** the hub deficit is majority-circumstance (Roemer:
  compensation owed in principle — flagged, not priced; no mechanism
  exists to pay it). Effort/luck remain entangled (no effort measure —
  the residual is honest, not residual-as-effort).
- **NOT LICENSED:** founder-blaming readings of hub failure (64% is
  the patch, not the agent); any claim separating effort from luck
  within arms (unmeasured, not zero).
- No bank, instrument, or verdict rescored. Cumulative worlds unchanged.
