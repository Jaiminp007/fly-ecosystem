# Fixed deterministic-taste continuation

The long-B factual census found local cue discrimination but near-zero net
food energy and high mortality. On each original long-B observe checkpoint at
the end of A (after the identical motor-freeze setup), compare continued
stochastic feeding with one fixed deterministic use of the same learned taste
estimate: accept exactly when its acceptance estimate is greater than 0.5.
For absent odor both accept. The threshold expresses the equal-sign decision
boundary, not a fitted label or sweep; it does not imply a safety guarantee.

Both arms compute and pay for the same estimate, the same threshold test and
the same existing RNG draw; the control executes the draw and the candidate
executes the threshold. Factual taste credit must record the actual action and
own observed outcome. No target valence, future or privileged phase label is
used. Model states, food, bodies, original debt and environment match at the
fork; no refill or revival. Retain the 512/1024/512/256 additional diagnostic
schedule and continue each body through B, C and odor-free transfer if alive.

Record full compressed traces, snapshots, receipts, world energy and deaths;
check whether the intervention changes B net intake and later survival before
considering its impact on saved A/B retention, pressure and transfer controls.
The fixed old histories are outcome-informed and not independent samples. This
hard-coded use of a learned estimate is not a newly acquired skill and cannot
replace the original schedule, the mandatory pressure/retention criteria or
fresh qualification. Preserve all failures and closed reserves.
