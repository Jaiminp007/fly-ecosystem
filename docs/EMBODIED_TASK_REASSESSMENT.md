# Task and action authority after the sparse-policy screen

Continuation: the opt-in environment and controls below have now been implemented and the [separate action-authority diagnostic passed](CUE_ACTION_AUTHORITY_RESULTS.md), including exact cold replay and independent arithmetic. This is no learned-skill result. The original not-yet-implemented statements below record the earlier design decision; current learning/retention qualification and later recursive stages remain open.

This is a post-result source audit and next-design decision, not another qualification result. The original fixed screen and every subsequent failed bank remain closed. It does not establish the cause of their failure or show that the environment is unlearnable.

The [phase source](../learning/continual-regimes.mjs) changes resource layouts at phase entry, shifts renewal centers during B, and lowers the renewal multiplier in C. Within B it moves renewal centers without moving existing food. All entry operations preserve existing energy. The [engine](../engine.mjs) applies the multiplier to **regrowth**, while consuming any food patch transfers its amount directly into body energy. The tasks contain no learned odor-to-quality associations or differing food valences. Lower renewal does not imply that C immediately has low inventory. This restates source behavior, not a new environmental experiment.

The [controller adapter](../learning/embodied-control.mjs) gives the external learner one of two speed levels every eight ticks while keeping the inherited steering scaffold and other outputs. Permitted observation/history lacks a phase label and direct renewal-rate/food-quality information. The earlier locked intervention showed hindsight action headroom, while accessible headroom from the permitted observations remained unproved. Neither that hindsight advantage nor these source facts proves that a speed-only learner can discover separately retainable skills in this schedule. The repeated failed gates do not justify another rate or replay search.

The project also already contained dopamine-like reward modulation in the [trace actor](../learning/trace-actor.mjs). Sparse expansion and fast/slow synaptic storage were a substantive new architecture relative to recent SARSA variants, but its failure is not evidence against mushroom bodies as biological learning systems.

The next direction to specify is an **embodied cue-valence foraging environment**, keeping the chosen circuit fixed initially while making a sensory association causally relevant to physical energy. Odor patterns must be external observable cues, not task IDs or direct edible/toxic labels. Their arbitrary outcome assignments must be randomized before observations and hidden from learner code. Bodies must navigate and pay actual movement, metabolism, storage and computation costs; reward remains actual net-energy change. Any harmful-food mechanism needs a complete physical energy/debt ledger. This would be a new environmental hypothesis, never a reinterpretation or repair of the old failed screen.

Before qualifying a learner, a separate fixed headroom diagnostic should require a value-informed evaluation-only controller to outperform cue-blind controls, demonstrating that permitted actions can affect energy in the new task. Its privileged information must never reach candidate learning. Matched cue-blind and independently shuffled-cue controls should test whether an eventual learning gain depends on the association. Null assignments should detect reward or label leakage. No parameter or assignment may be selected for candidate performance on that diagnostic bank.

The subsequent prospective qualification must retain continuing bodies and all-founder denominators, use distinct independently registered A/B/C cue families and new resource histories, and expose shared finite synapses to later learning. Track actual cross-task active-synapse overlap and receipt overflow; neither raw parameter count nor a buffer size alone proves interference pressure. Require separate new-skill acquisition, positive earlier-skill-over-reset, separate 95%-advantage retention, whole-sequence energy superiority, nonnegative survival guard means, all physical ledgers, cold replay and independent plasticity/inference audits. Add transfer checks on the original resource task so an artificial conditioning result cannot be described as solving the earlier failed ecology. No task reset, energy refill, easier threshold or broader RSI claim follows a pass.

This source audit changes the next research action from another learner variant to a preregistered action-authority and cue-learning task design. The environment, sensor interface and controls have **not yet been implemented or tested**. Persistent autonomous revisions and copied-improvement/search-productivity experiments remain later obligations after a qualified embodied substrate is established.
