# Odor-routed motor candidate

The failed motor acquired only in cue tasks and then entered an odor-free,
all-beneficial original ecology. Its C weights often prefer slow movement on
transfer states; fixed full speed improves the seven available forks but does
not pass the survivor gate. A 16-transition factual-return census does not
establish that a longer horizon alone fixes the problem.

One prospective candidate uses two independent copies of the existing
three-speed motor, selected solely by the body's current observable odor
presence: present or absent. Each copy has its own weights, eligibility,
reservoir and RNG and learns only from its owner's executed actions and budget
energy transitions. A change in observed presence discards and counts an
unresolved transition rather than inventing cross-bank credit; eligibility is
cleared when a bank is exited. Both banks and all routing work must be charged.
The cue taste learner remains separate. A sensor-based route is engineered
structure, not itself a learned skill or phase/timer identifier.

To give the absent bank actual factual experience, one proposed acquisition
curriculum retains the three 512-tick cue phases A/B/C, then uses a fourth
256-tick odor-free original-ecology phase with `resourceSeeds[3]`. Bodies,
resources, debt and learned state continue without refill or revival. The
frozen transfer probe uses the separate existing `probeSeeds.transfer`, never
the acquisition seed. Earlier A/B/C checkpoints remain available for
same-body retention testing after the fourth phase. This deliberately changes
the training curriculum; it must be reported and applied identically to all
matched candidate and control arms, without weakening original transfer,
functional-pressure, survival or qualification gates. If a founder or population
dies, no substitute checkpoint may be invented.

Before development outcomes, independently verify actual per-founder present
and absent action support, routing from lawful observations, boundary/terminal
credit, serialized continuation, complete state/work/debt billing and matched
equal-experience controls including reset, frozen, matched native/guarded and
cheap native-full. The original retained-skill and fresh qualification tests
must remain intact. This fixed candidate is a development hypothesis, not a
result, and no counterfactual fork labels enter training.

## Prototype status

`learning/odor-routed-motor.mjs` implements the two own-experience banks and
explicitly drops unresolved cross-route transitions. `learning/odor-routed-taste.mjs`
connects them to the existing factual taste-credit mechanism and a shared
chargeable parent state. Nine focused tests pass for context switches, actual
forced actions, terminal credit, freeze/reset, serializable continuation,
odor-free feeding and the existing motor core.

`learning/odor-routed-experiment.mjs` now integrates the banks into the physical
cue world and fourth odor-free acquisition phase. It captures a real D endpoint
only if that phase reaches its boundary, and supports same-body saved-model,
reset and matched-control forks. All eight reused histories have a distinct
odor-free acquisition and transfer seed. Thirteen focused tests pass for shared
physical starts, actual absent-route experience, present-weight preservation,
encoded state, terminal debt, checkpoint restore, reset/frozen controls, model
copying and the fourth-phase boundary.

These are synthetic engineering checks; the synthetic state-billing test is not
an independent full cost audit. No population-level action support, survival,
retention, transfer or qualification claim follows. Captured-source synthetic
routing, learning, cost and frozen-probe audits are recorded in
`docs/ODOR_ROUTED_ENGINEERING_AUDIT.md`; full-length development outcomes and
their independent audits remain outstanding.
