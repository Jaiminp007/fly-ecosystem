## Predeclared gates

Twenty-one comparisons cover nineteen required directional energy effects, one required null equivalence check and one optional trace-attribution effect. Required directional effects are B and C acquisition; whole-sequence superiority to eleven controls; separate initial A/B skill over reset; separate A/B retention of at least 95% of the initial advantage; and two original-ecology transfer effects. Retention is `postC - .95*savedSkill - .05*reset`, paired on common physical bodies. Trace attribution is candidate minus lambda-zero and cannot rescue failure elsewhere.

Development requires all nineteen directional means strictly positive, null candidate-minus-blind mean within +/-0.5 energy/founder, nonnegative observed mean survival effects versus all twelve non-null controls, and every history passing both manipulation checks. Pressure requires >=50% of A active synapses reused in B/C, >=50% of B reused in C, and at least half of original bodies exceeding sixteen completed factual receipts. Activity requires >=50% of updates with nonzero dopamine-like signal and positive absolute slow-synapse transfer. This is a go/no-go engineering screen, not qualification or a confidence-bound pass.

Only if development and both full audits pass may unchanged confirmation run. Sixteen reserved histories must clear positive simultaneous one-sided t bounds and exact paired sign-flip p <= `.05/21` for every required directional effect. The entire two-sided null interval, using `t.ppf(1-.05/(2*21),15)`, must lie within +/-0.5. All survival, pressure and activity gates remain required. Report individual effects and uncertainty; histories are the replicates. Equal observed survival is not a survival benefit or a population-level noninferiority proof.

Failure closes the confirmation reserve. No sample extension, seed replacement, alternate learner, parameter search, threshold relaxation, candidate selection or policy promotion follows this bank. A failed transfer or retention gate cannot be replaced with a weaker conditioning-only success claim. This is one human-proposed environment/circuit experiment, not an autonomous algorithm revision.
