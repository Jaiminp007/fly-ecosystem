# Matched factual gate replay

The retention factorial implicates saved-to-current gate changes on reused B
probes, but does not establish a replay remedy. Test exactly one phase-blind
gate update change: retain up to 32 factual own `(agentId, tick, features,
executed action, debt-adjusted reward)` records with reservoir sampling. Each
eligible observation performs the original current-record update and a second
update, using either the sampled reservoir record or the current record. Both
arms maintain the same reservoir and RNG streams, execute identical draws and
two updates, and pay identical declared per-observation selection/update work
and actual encoded memory storage. No stage labels, other bodies' samples,
counterfactual outcomes or hidden valence enter learning. Reward includes the
declared gate-observation work before either update, including on death.

Include the matched random-action and selector-shadow physical controls with
the same replay architecture, plus the existing original-ecology reset,
native, guarded and cheap native controls. Use the original eight reused
development histories and 512/512/512/256 schedule with motor frozen after A;
saved/current gate weights must follow the same A/B pressure and retention
probes. Require both actual gate actions for each D survivor in the learned and
random arms; apply the original 5% pressure, 95% retention, transfer and
survival requirements without adjustment. Compare factual replay versus
current-sample arms directly as well as against controls.

The four fixed arms are `fresh` (learned selection, current second update),
`reservoir` (learned selection, factual reservoir second update), `random`
(uniform selection, reservoir second update) and `shadow` (keep movement,
reservoir second update). The replay architecture charges 18 initialization
operations, 100 selection and three wrapper operations per eligible action,
230 observation operations during acquisition, and 70 during frozen probes;
reset also bills the encoded memory traversal. The observation work is
subtracted from the factual energy target before updating. All arms preserve
their buffers and selected weights in checkpoints, and frozen probes retain
their allocated memory unless reset explicitly clears it and pays for traversal.

Predeclare and capture the source, inputs, tapes, checkpoints and complete
debt/work/storage receipts. Independently reconstruct own transition provenance,
reservoir selection, both SGD updates, RNG streams, physical endpoints and
deterministic replay. Test isolation, reset/freeze, terminal work and memory
pricing before collection. Require 1 GiB free before collection and a 100 MiB
floor during collection. Outcomes are development evidence, not fresh
qualification or autonomous revision; keep all failed studies and reserves.
