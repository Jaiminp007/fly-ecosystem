# Frozen half-turn commitment feasibility diagnostic

Prospective exploratory design, October 25, 2026. Preserve every prior failure
and closed reserve. This is one bounded option witness, not learned transfer.

Use the eight attempt-15 end-C anchors and their original transfer seeds, all
24 original founders, inherited physical state and debt, and the original
256-tick endpoint. Full-speed native steering with acceptance is the reference.
The food selector ignores reachability; rejected movement still costs energy.
A remembered escape may suppress attraction long enough to leave an obstacle.
No hidden geometry, collision flag or target identity may select the event.

Select at most one event per history: earliest pre-step decision in 17--112
where a living founder has sixteen consecutive preceding raw observations with
food proximity >0 and previous intake <=0, and a wall warning in the preceding
four. Break simultaneous ties by smallest founder ID. This unchanged prior
stall trigger is weak evidence of trapping; report observer collision/target
annotations without excluding cases. Missing events remain in the denominator.

The focal founder commands turn=+1, full speed and acceptance for exactly
ceil(pi/(2.8*.1))=12 decisions, then returns to native-full. This nominal half
turn is derived from the actuator equation. No direction, duration, threshold,
second-window or trigger sweep follows outcomes. Other founders cruise.

Compare with matched native-full and bare native-full from identical starts.
The two matched executors reserve the same countdown/mode fields, compute the
same recommendation, and activate the same countdown at the selected event;
only one executes it. Bill sixteen extra scalar operations per decision for the
countdown selector and conservative raw-history update, storing a no-intake
counter and a four-bit wall history in both; charge encoded state and
the full inherited shadow learner in both. Settle all completed observations.
Observer selection is an event-fork diagnostic, not a deployable multi-body
policy; no shared observer history is given to a body or called learned memory.
Bare cruise retains its existing cheaper architecture and disclosed final-work
settlement difference; do not weaken that benchmark.

Record at option end, 64 elapsed ticks and endpoint: cohort energy/debt/survival,
focal and other-body budgets, intake and charged physical costs. Preserve full
traces and the selected pre-step checkpoint. Check exact no-intervention restore
and observer neutrality. Different deaths may change shared RNG draw assignment.

The descriptive go rule requires positive mean endpoint energy over BOTH full
references, at least six of eight histories positive over each reference, and
nonnegative summed survivor differences over both. Missing events contribute
zero commitment effect against matched cruise, not removal. This is not a
statistical qualification gate. A pass motivates checking A/B/C exposure and a
matched learned option selector; it does not establish acquisition, retention,
functional pressure, transfer or RSI. A failure closes only this exact witness.
