# Balanced replay founder-level pressure census

For each arm and reused history, group D survivors by whether their own cue
taste learner received at least one factual B-phase example, computed from
the saved A and B `seen` counts. On the existing A and B saved-A/saved-B
probes, reconstruct each founder's final physical energy minus outstanding
debt and aggregate own beneficial-intake-minus-toxin receipts. Calculate A
pressure as saved-A minus saved-B and B acquisition as saved-B minus saved-A.
Verify identical paired initial worlds, starting energies and debts; verify
that sums across all founders reproduce recorded endpoints and receipt totals.
Include bodies that died during probes and fixed debt for earlier deaths.

Report grouped totals, per-history counts, positive/negative body counts and
means without treating founders within a shared world as independent samples.
The groups are selected by own experience and later D survival and may be
confounded by health, geometry, competition or random trajectories. This is
an outcome-informed decomposition of total-world interventions, not a causal
effect of exposure, a qualification test, or a reason to weaken any gate.
Verify hashes and reproduce aggregate arithmetic independently. No new rollout
or reserve is opened.
