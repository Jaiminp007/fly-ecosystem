# Extended-B factual taste census

Read the existing compressed B tapes from both arms, with exact event-hash
checks. Split each B phase into its fixed first and second 512-tick halves.
On each actual decision with an unambiguous local contact cue, compare the
body's recorded feeding propensity and realized acceptance for evaluator-only
beneficial versus harmful codebook cues. Link each decision to its actual
physical receipt and retain separate intake/toxin totals. Do not provide
valence, codebooks, imagined counterfactuals or phase labels to any learner.

These are repeated, selected observations on interacting worlds, not
independent samples, causal effects or proof that the encountered patch was
the only food eaten after motion. Missing late observations and survivor bias
must remain visible. The comparison diagnoses whether a proposed energy-saving
intervention should target taste discrimination or another mechanism; it does
not change the failed extended schedule or the original qualification gates.
