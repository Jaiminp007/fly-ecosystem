# Balanced B-phase own-credit funnel

For each rbase/blend D survivor in the eight reused development histories,
reconstruct B-phase decision/observation pairs from the recorded acquisition
tape. Count the nested sequence: decisions, stationary, stationary plus
accept, plus odor, plus contact, plus homogeneous contact, plus nonzero own
`lastFood`. The final stage must equal the saved B-minus-A `cueTaste.seen`
count. For zero-example survivors, classify the earliest stage never reached;
also report the experienced group and per-history counts. Verify source and
trace hashes, decision/observation pairing, per-body credit and aggregates.

This outcome-informed, D-survivor-selected descriptive funnel cannot show
which intervention would produce useful B samples or protect transfer, and it
does not infer rejected or unobserved labels. Bodies share worlds and are not
independent replicates. Do not force actions, weaken gates or open a reserve.
