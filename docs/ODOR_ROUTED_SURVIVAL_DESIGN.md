# Frozen-versus-continuing survival diagnosis

The outcome-informed development study ends with 20 continuously learning
founders versus 60 frozen-after-A founders. Freezing changes learning updates,
exploration, controller work and later trajectories together. This difference
does not by itself isolate any one cause.

Use only the eight existing learned and frozen-after-A acquisition tapes.
Verify complete trace hashes and compare every recorded event through tick 512
using independent prefix hashes. For each arm and phase A/B/C/odor-free D,
reconstruct actual decisions, speed and route counts, acceptance, food and toxin
receipts, total paid physical/controller cost and lineage survival at each
boundary. Confirm receipt totals against saved phase statistics and separately
report per-history rows. Compute total controller energy and end debt from the
saved accounting state. No revived bodies, substitute checkpoints, new seeds or
counterfactual observations are allowed.

These are factual descriptions of a randomized interacting world. After the
freeze, arms can consume different foods, die at different times and change RNG
consumption; raw differences are not individual causal estimates of updating or
exploration. If the A prefixes agree, that establishes a matched pre-treatment
history for the recorded events, not an independently simulated physical replay.
The result cannot qualify learning, retention, transfer or RSI and must not
weaken the existing gates. Any follow-on intervention must fix its mechanism and
matched cost/behavior controls before collecting new outcomes.
