# Transfer-stage steering diagnosis

The fixed reused-world plan in `DECOUPLED_TRANSFER_STAGE_PLAN.md` compares
saved B, C and D steering with the same D taste, A motors, transfer seed,
world, inherited debt and 326 paid component writes per survivor.

| Contrast | Energy/founder | Positive histories | Net intake/founder | Positive histories | Survivor difference |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| C minus B | -0.349703 | 3/8 | -0.227525 | 3/8 | 0 |
| D minus C | -0.007707 | 4/8 | -0.189525 | 3/8 | 0 |
| D minus B | -0.357410 | 2/8 | -0.417050 | 1/8 | 0 |

Every history has equal survivors across these arms. The mean transfer-energy
decline is already present by the C checkpoint, although histories differ and
the net-intake mean also declines from C to D. These are outcome-informed,
total-world checkpoint interventions, not proof of general transfer or of
which events within C caused the drift.

For all 62 D survivors, B-to-C checkpoint comparisons show unchanged
absent-cue steering banks and changed shared steering weights. All 47,412
recorded transfer decisions have zero own cue and select the absent route.
Given the current additive shared-plus-route steering law, the B/C difference
in the evaluated transfer policy therefore comes from shared-weight changes,
not changes to the absent bank; changed behavior can still alter interactions
and random trajectories. This is a mechanism-specific target for a learned,
paid interference-control approach, not justification to freeze B weights or
choose a checkpoint by hidden phase.

Evidence: `evidence/decoupled-transfer-stage/20261026-v1`. Independent
assignment, ledger and replay checks cover 186 copied models, 24 runs and
47,412 decisions/receipts. `scripts/check-decoupled-transfer-route.mjs`
separately checks input and trace hashes, own cues and checkpoint vectors.
Neither checker independently simulates physics or proves actual hardware
energy costs. Qualification, reset/cheap-control comparisons and the later
autonomous revision objective remain unmet.

Next investigate factual, phase-blind protection of the shared steering model
from contradictory later updates, while continuing to learn C and absent-cue
behavior. Any proposed mechanism must account for memory, inference and update
work, preserve genuine A/B pressure and retention, and pass the original
transfer controls and fresh audit. Do not promote a post-hoc checkpoint.
