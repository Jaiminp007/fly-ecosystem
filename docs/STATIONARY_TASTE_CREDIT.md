# Prospective stationary taste-credit hypothesis

This is a human-proposed follow-up to the audited failed direct-value study,
not an autonomous algorithm revision or a qualified candidate. No performance
seeds, parameters or replacement bank are selected here.

The previous learner fits bootstrapped budget-adjusted energy returns. In its
audited factual bank most completed updates have negative reward and recorded
action frequencies remain broadly spread. That description does not identify
the cause of failure. A different hypothesis is to acquire an auxiliary odor
outcome model directly from the body's existing signed intake sensation, while
continuing to score every physical cost, toxin loss, debt and death honestly.

`engine.mjs` already sets `lastFood` to the body's clipped net intake in cue
worlds, and exposes it in sensor coordinate 13. Its sign is an experienced
bodily outcome, not the world's hidden patch-valence label. No observer labels,
patch identity/amount, another body, task phase or desired action enter learning.
Making this sensor available immediately after the action is an explicitly new
feedback-timing intervention; the old protocols and observations are unchanged.

The first isolated component admits a taste example only after **accept and
remain stationary**, with a pre-action local contact cue and a nonzero own
signed intake sensation. A neutral sensation provides no valence example.
Movement can change which patch is consumed, so cruise experiences are not
silently labeled with their pre-movement cue. The record concerns net experienced
intake, including supported mixed-contact cases; it is not proof of a single
patch's latent valence. Terminal experiences are retained once and never revive
a body. The source action's cue/tick is retained across regime boundaries.

The component requires a disclosed contact-present bit in addition to odor.
The current contact function falls back to a distant native odor; that fallback
must not masquerade as local taste evidence. Future integration must compute
and physically meter the local query, the bit, temporary storage, processing,
auxiliary model, inference, updates and any replay. The current component does
not query the world, control a body or alter an energy ledger.

This narrows a lawful credit mechanism before committing to a learner campaign.
It does **not** specify a complete learner, movement policy, replay method,
memory-pressure intervention or fresh qualification protocol. Those obligations
must be completed and fixed before a new performance bank. An auxiliary taste
fit alone cannot establish useful behavior or original-ecology transfer, where
all food is beneficial and the always-accept cruise comparator remains required.

Any later study must retain distinct A/B acquisition, later learning, separate
95% retention, demonstrated functional interference, nonnegative exact survivor
guards, all original founders/debts, both transfer acceptance comparators,
frozen/reset/blind/shuffled/null controls, fresh exclusions and both full audits.
Do not weaken these gates, rerun the failed candidate unchanged, tune on closed
outcomes or open a failed reserve. Two persistent autonomous algorithm revisions
and copied-improvement/matched-budget search tests remain subsequent stages.
