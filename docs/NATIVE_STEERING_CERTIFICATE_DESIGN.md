# Conservative frozen native-action certificate

The two focal crossovers implicate paid control work on their observed paths,
but do not justify an energy threshold or replacing a learned policy. Test one
exact early-exit opportunity: a frozen cue-shared steering policy can select
native without computing rich spatial points if either wall sensor is nonzero
(the existing guard forbids midpoint), or an upper bound proves that action 1
cannot exceed action 0 for any possible cue and legal remaining spatial
features. This bound is independent of hidden phase, patch identity and future
outcomes. It preserves the original strict-tie preference for native.

For each of the 17 cue banks, combine its saved residual with the saved shared
weights. The first six steering features are available from raw sensors and
current own budget energy before the rich spatial observation. Bound feature 6
in [0,1] and feature 7 in [-1,1]. The upper action-value difference is the
known-feature contribution plus `max(0, delta6) + abs(delta7)`. Require every
bank's upper bound to be strictly negative after a deliberately conservative
floating-point margin; uncertain or invalid values must fall back to the full
original computation. This is an engineered proof of an existing action, not
a new policy, learned skill or guarantee that the energy savings rescue anyone.

The fixed certificate's arithmetic and retained scratch bytes are charged.
First verify synthetic random, tie, wall, boundary and invalid-input cases
against the exact frozen policy. Then replay both full and residual-only
captured transfer worlds as an observer, verify every certificate against the
actual recorded steering action, and estimate same-state work headroom. The
estimate must subtract certificate work and scratch byte-time, include setup
and state costs, and must not call uncomputed physical outcomes a pass. Any
later embodied integration must preserve factual action/RNG semantics, inherited
debt, terminal charges and unchanged strong controls. Closed reserves stay shut.

For the fixed offline estimate, subtract certificate work on every decision,
two proposed clearing operations on a certified decision, and the byte-time
of a persistent `nativeProof` scratch field on every decision. Credit only the
known minimum `2 + 9 * pointCount` observation work, the measured original
selector work, and 15 guard operations when certified. Ignore any saving from
discarding existing point/index storage or other work. These are conservative
declared-arithmetic estimates, not a physical-run or machine-energy result.
