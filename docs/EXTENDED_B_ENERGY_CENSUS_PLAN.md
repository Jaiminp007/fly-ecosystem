# Extended-B phase energy census

Independently read the already captured compressed B and C acquisition tapes
for both arms and eight development histories. Verify every uncompressed trace
hash, event count and matching start/final physical checkpoint. Reconcile each
phase's actual physical energy from starting live bodies, physical receipts,
age-death discarded energy and surviving ending bodies. Count energy versus
age deaths and report beneficial intake, toxin damage, total physical cost and
the paid learner-cost subset. Preserve unpaid debt separately.

This is a retrospective descriptive census of interacting trajectories, not a
counterfactual simulation or proof that any cost or action caused an individual
death. Receipt `paidExtra` is a declared accounting quantity, not a physical
hardware measurement. No labels feed back to learning, no evidence is removed,
and the failed extended schedule, original benchmark and closed reserves remain.
