# PRE-TRANSFER COMPATIBILITY V1 (SLT-inspired, read-only)

Date: 2026-11-02. Zero worlds. Licensed as measurement by R310
(Dipu/SLT: directional pre-invasion compatibility orders forgetting).
N=117 transplant links (C4–C13 frozen rows; C2/C3 lack digests).

## Metric (pre-registered in-script)

Overlap = fraction of shared cues with identical labels between the
resident-train row (H0, pre-transfer) and the invader-world
(the transferred row's own observed cue labels, H1 — world facts,
not learned weights). Both pre-transfer observables; no
post-learning contamination. Outcome = link's flippedHB
correctness (frozen scorer). Variance exists (overlap 0.11–0.89,
median 0.55) — the metric is not degenerate.

## Result: NO stable relationship

- Pooled r(overlap, hb) = **+0.106** (n=117). Tertile means:
  0.323 / 0.321 / 0.351 — flat.
- Per-line r flips sign: C5 +0.64, C7 +0.47, C8 +0.80, C11 +0.89
  vs C9 −0.95, C12 −0.47, C13 −0.43, C10 −0.02 (n=9–12 each).
  The extremes sit on the smallest variances (C9 flat-low HB) —
  noise signatures, not effects. No stable direction exists.
- Establishment secondary: r(resident same-val, transfer) = −0.12.
  How well the resident learned its own task predicts nothing
  about transfer either.

## Reading (two branches, both honest)

1. Compatibility truly doesn't order OUR transfer. SLT's
   resident/invader framing assumes forgetting-by-displacement;
   our wound is directional (HB extinction failure) and
   dose-shaped, not compatibility-shaped. The whole arc agrees:
   nothing about the SOURCE predicts the wound (dose, leg, and
   now overlap all fail as predictors; only the DIRECTION matters).
2. Our overlap is too coarse to be SLT's compatibility (cue-label
   agreement vs their directional pre-invasion measure). A finer
   compatibility (weight-space, gradient-alignment) might work —
   but it needs record-level machinery that doesn't exist (OPEN
   CELLS §3/§4), and the pooled flatness gives no reason to build it.

Either branch: the predictor our tax work lacks is STILL lacking,
and SLT-compatibility isn't it (on this evidence). No verdict
moves (all NOGO/INVALID stand; this was measurement, never a line).
