# Factual A-probe prediction comparison

On the existing AA A-probe tapes for each shadow and stop D world, pair each
actual own decision with its own observed outcome. For odor-present,
homogeneous-contact, stationary accepted decisions with nonzero food signal,
evaluate the same observed cue with the saved AA and BA taste models at probe
start. Group by the observed positive or negative own signal, and record event
counts and mean B-minus-A acceptance-probability and shared/residual logit
differences. Reconstruct predictions and verify the observed AA probability.
The BA prediction is an offline evaluation, not a BA physical outcome or a
label supplied to the learner. Count events and bodies separately; repeated
events, selection on AA acceptance, D survival and shared worlds make them
dependent. No hidden patch valence or fresh reserve is used.

Check original compressed trace hashes, matching initial worlds, model ancestry,
decision/observation pairing, source hashes and summary arithmetic independently.
This describes factual AA trajectories only; it cannot determine physical
counterfactual performance, qualify a skill or loosen any research gate.
