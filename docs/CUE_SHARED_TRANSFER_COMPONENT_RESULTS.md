# Cue-shared transfer component intervention

The fixed post-outcome intervention masked saved shared or absent-residual
weights on the same eight D worlds; all four arms paid the same 16 per-body
setup writes. These are total-world effects with changing competition, debt,
guard decisions and RNG trajectories—not fixed-neighbor individual effects or
training labels.

| Intervention versus | Mean energy / founder | Positive histories | Survivor difference |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Shared-only versus full | +0.299265 | 4/8 | 0 |
| Residual-only versus full | +0.263982 | 5/8 | -1 |
| Shared-only versus matched native | +0.276294 | 5/8 | +1 |
| Residual-only versus matched native | +0.241011 | 7/8 | 0 |

The residual-only comparison shows a possible total-world headroom against
the matched native architecture, not a qualifying acquired skill. A separate
**post-outcome**, unprespecified comparison to the already recorded same-world
cheap native-full control gives shared-only +0.143380/founder, 4/8 wins, -1
survivor; residual-only +0.108098, 3/8 wins, -2 survivors. Neither meets the
unchanged transfer consistency or survival requirement. The original learned
transfer and functional-pressure failures remain failures; no mask is promoted.

Captured sources, matched starts, full traces, endpoints and predeclared
comparisons are in `evidence/cue-shared-transfer-components/20261026-v1`.
The readback independently checks 224 assignments and setup charges, 56,179
decision/receipt pairs and all 32 captured-source replays. The separately
labelled post-hoc cheap-control comparison verifies common world hashes and
references the earlier study, not independent samples. Neither audit
independently simulates physics or establishes complete low-level cost
adequacy. Closed reserves, fresh qualification and RSI remain untouched.
