## Why this line, and only this one
BIHOME showed return-practice dissolves even the recombined edge
(bi frac −0.07, naive overtakes all) — immersion is the universal
solvent for exact, mutated, and recombined transmission. The social
channel is law-grade on the natal task (6/6 pairs) with a different
mechanism (adopt winners, escape failing lines) that might resist
catch-up differently: social newborns already track the winners, so
return-practice may have less to teach them. The licensed question:
does the social edge survive gauntlet + return where all three
asocial modes dissolved? Same MUTHOME pipeline with a social birth
colony (copyconditional, trained founders) + copy + naivekid arms.
Not a re-run of MUTHOME/MUTHEIR/BIHOME (different mode) or SOCIAL
(no tasks).

INVALID), skill pair 256 (skillT > skillN else INCOMPLETE-NOSKILL),

pre-probes 256; BCR-train 1280 (learn ON, engine B-entry, auto

social gap after return + social retention fraction vs birth.
