# Niche fingerprint — weights identify their niche perfectly; one test segment erases half

Date: 2026-10-26. Read-only (zero new worlds; frozen train-end weight
vectors, 30 niches × 24 agents + V1i transferred-end rows). Adami's
prescription (round 38): model-quality = mutual information with the
niche. Operationalized as 1-NN leave-one-agent-out niche identification
(Euclidean on z-scored 16-dim taste weights).

## 1. Pre-test: accuracy 1.000 (720/720) vs chance 0.033

Every agent's weight vector is nearer its 23 niche-mates than any of
696 outsiders — across 30 banks, 8 turn-gate lines + VP + gain-dose.
No run-mate artifact is possible: the only factors agents share within
a run (world layout, codebook, resources, trajectory statistics) are
ALL niche components; options/ticks are identical everywhere and cannot
drive differential matching. The weights ARE models of their niche
(the linear bits read out the codebook almost directly).

## 2. Post-test retention: 57% still match own train niche (vs 3% chance)

V1i transferred-end weights (train-carried + 1792 test ticks) vs the
30-niche reference: own-train matches 7/24, 15/24, 19/24 per pair
(mean 41/72 = 0.57); test-niche matches 0/72 — expected degenerate
(no reference weights were ever trained on test codebooks, so the test
side has no template; reported as method limit, not finding).

One test segment erases ~half the train fingerprint (100% → 57%) while
12–24% leak to other train niches. Retention is substantial AND
partial — the first direct number on WHAT-RETAINS' question from
frozen rows: behavior transfers (HB 0.31–0.42, BH 0.72–0.84) on
weights that still read mostly-train. The transfer tax has a weight
signature: carried structure dominates end-state weights.

## 3. Mating implication: fidelity proven, selection missing

Transplant IS exact copy: fingerprint starts at 100% by construction.
V1's null (16/16 identical, gap 0) therefore CANNOT be information loss
— transmission fidelity is ~perfect, and the mating track's problem is
selection/compounding (no differential, no compounding window), exactly
as Crispo's assimilation conditions require (round 28). Prescription
for any mating revival (instrumentation, not executed): snapshot
weights in mating rows — fingerprint-preservation under mutation then
becomes directly measurable (does σ=0.05 destroy transmitted knowledge,
or preserve it while selection stays absent?).

## 4. Bank-dependence link (bounded)

Banks ARE distinguishable in weight space (necessary condition for
bank-effects met). But the fingerprint does not explain HB variance:
carried priors are saturated everywhere (0.96–0.98), and remap geometry
is weak (r=+0.23 wrong-signed). HB pair variance (0.16–0.47) remains
unexplained; BH needs no explanation (uniformly fine). Cumulative
unchanged (zero new worlds). No RSI claim.
