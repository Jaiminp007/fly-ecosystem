# Reproductive learning study

The fixed factorial study completed 96 worlds. The joint learning-signal gate **failed**; no ecological promotion follows.

The protocol crossed acquired memory on/off with mutation on/off, matched founders in fixed-renewal and relocating-food environments, and retained natural reproduction and lifespan. The table shows learning minus memory-disabled paired mean differences and recorded seed-level 95% intervals.

| Environment | Mutation | Population-time per founder | Births per founder | Ending population |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| fixedRenewal | mutation | 0.130 [-0.062, 0.323] | 0.521 [-0.244, 1.286] | 1.167 [-7.751, 10.084] |
| fixedRenewal | fixed | 0.259 [0.114, 0.405] | 1.219 [0.660, 1.777] | 9.167 [0.626, 17.707] |
| relocation | mutation | 0.150 [-0.072, 0.373] | 0.757 [-0.342, 1.856] | 5.500 [-9.547, 20.547] |
| relocation | fixed | 0.278 [0.126, 0.429] | 1.389 [0.668, 2.110] | 12.000 [3.156, 20.844] |

The fixed-genome comparisons supported benefits in both environments, but the mutation-enabled comparisons remained uncertain. The predeclared combined gate required every cell to pass and failed. Mutation-off is a distinct condition; its favorable results do not qualify the full study.

The audit checked 13075 births with fresh acquired state, 17 extinctions, 0 capacity-censored worlds, sampled demographic/energy arithmetic, matched founders, and all paired intervals. 8 selected cold physical replays reproduced complete state and midpoint continuation. The maximum sampled absolute accounting residual was 5.7632860261946917e-08. Only the first fixed seed in every arm and environment received an independent physical replay.
