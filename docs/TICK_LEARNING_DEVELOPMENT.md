# Fixed tick learner: prospective development and confirmation protocol

This document specifies one human-proposed candidate, not an autonomous revision.
The protocol/collector/scorer are in preparation. No performance seed bank is
registered by this document. Do not collect fresh outcomes until complete
engineering, independent inference and captured-source checks pass and the
source closure, exclusion inputs and fresh histories have been frozen together.

## Candidate, worlds and observations

Use the unchanged 161-parameter tick actor-critic, initialization, rates, reward,
traces, epsilon mixture and four-action menu in `TICK_ACTOR_CRITIC_DESIGN.md`.
The current input is 17 raw lawful own sensors plus 16 exact odor coordinates.
There is no task ID, hidden valence, factual archive, shared experience, oracle
codebook or supplied gradient. All training transitions are own one-tick
budget-adjusted energy intervals. The last nonterminal interval remains pending
at phase boundaries. No performance tuning or candidate search is permitted.

Every history has 24 original founders and the same continuing A512 / B512 /
C512 / return-A256 schedule. No body/energy/food refill, replacement founder,
reproduction or mutation occurs. Resource-family entry preserves inventory and
physical RNG; measured inventory/supply, not a parameter name, describes scarcity.
Thirteen arms are candidate, frozen-after-A, reset-at-B/C, cue-blind, shuffled
odor, null candidate/blind, native incumbent, four fixed menu actions and uniform
random. All matched policies use the same lawful inputs/cadence/motor interface.
Only the separately disclosed native reference retains incumbent acquisition.
Fixed policies have no unused learner circuit and pay their actual work/storage.

## Common-body probes and pressure

Use the candidate end-C body checkpoint for fifteen distinct frozen probes:
the original fourteen specifications plus A with end-B weights. Probe length is
256 ticks. Each family shares physical bodies, body RNG, resource seed, counters
and inherited all-founder debt. Replace only model weights. Clear eligibility
and pending outcomes, disable acquisition and preserve the training source.
Add an exact copy/reinstall A:A sham, with an identical event tape/result.
Reuse overlapping pressure comparisons; do not execute a duplicate battery.

The original all-beneficial transfer ecology has no odor. Require end-C to
beat reset, always-accept cruising and always-accept stationary. Saved-A transfer
is descriptive only. A missing end-C checkpoint produces explicit all-dead
zero incremental probes; it cannot pass positive skill/acquisition gates.
Extinction and terminal unpaid work are retained, without selected-survivor means.

Prices remain `operationEnergy=5e-6/17` and
`byteEnergyPerSecond=1e-5/(17*8)`. All active canonical encoded state and actual
metered policy work are billed in physical energy; unpaid costs remain debt.
Every probe value is surviving energy minus starting energy minus debt change,
divided by all original founders. Initialization is performed only for living
reset bodies. Honest charged endpoints are retained; every inferential skill,
retention and transfer-reset comparison conservatively adds the disclosed reset
initialization energy back to the reset value. Construction cost alone is not
acquired skill. This is an abstract cost model, not biological metabolism/heap.

Functional pressure follows `TICK_FUNCTIONAL_PRESSURE.md`: reinstating end-B
weights in common A bodies must lose more than five percent of the acquired A
advantage and worsen net beneficial intake less toxin damage. Acquired A/B
skills and useful B acquisition are independently required. End-C must still
recover/retain at least 95 percent of A and B learned advantages. This tests
actual transient functional interference along the continuing trajectory,
not hard memory-capacity saturation. It is not assumed to occur or to pass.

## Fixed inference family

Use eight fresh development histories and sixteen distinct reserved confirmation
histories. A history is the independent inference unit; bodies, probes, repeated
shams and ticks are not additional samples. The complete family has 22 required
tests, with no optional gate allowed to repair a required failure:

- Ten whole-sequence energy comparisons versus frozen, reset, cue-blind,
  shuffled, native incumbent, four fixed menu policies and random.
- B acquisition (B:B minus B:A) and C acquisition (C:C minus C:B).
- Separate acquired A:A and B:B advantages over corrected reset.
- Separate A/B 95-percent retention: current C minus .95 times the saved
  acquired model minus .05 times corrected reset, in that family's common bodies.
- Three original-ecology end-C transfer comparisons: corrected reset and both
  same-interface always-accept translation modes.
- Substantive functional interference and separate physical food interference.
- Null candidate-minus-blind equivalence within +/-0.5 energy per founder.

Development requires every directional mean strictly positive, null mean strictly
inside its margin, every survival guard nonnegative and activity in every history.
Development does not establish qualification. A failure closes the entire reserve.
Before opening confirmation, complete all development numerical and exact physical
replay audits against the captured source/result hashes. No tuning, reselection,
extension, bank substitution or opening an older failed candidate's reserve.

Confirmation requires every directional one-sided Student-t lower bound strictly
positive and exact paired sign-flip p <= .05/22. The null entire two-sided interval
using t at `1-.05/44` must lie strictly within +/-0.5. Directional critical is t
at `1-.05/22`; df is histories minus one. Exact sign flips enumerate all 2^16
assignments, with the existing 1e-12 sum-comparison tolerance. Student-t inference
assumes adequate mean uncertainty estimation; the sign-flip test assumes symmetric
independent seed-level paired effects. These assumptions are stated, not proved
by software tests. Bonferroni covers the complete declared family.

Nineteen mean-survival guards cover the ten sequence comparisons plus the nine
acquisition, initial-skill, retention and transfer probe comparisons. Retention
compares end-C survivors to the saved acquired model; it does not average in
reset survivors. Sum paired integer survivor differences and test that integer
sum >=0, then divide by founders/histories for reporting. No epsilon or float
cancellation decides ties. Report descriptive 95-percent t intervals separately;
equal counts do not demonstrate a survival benefit. Every development/confirmation
history additionally needs at least twelve original candidate bodies with actual
updates and a parameter change exceeding 1e-12 from initialization. Activity
is not evidence of pressure or useful learning.
