# Own-steering development results

The fixed randomized-acquisition development comparison fails the unchanged
transfer gate. One of eight learned populations became extinct before its C
checkpoint, so its C-dependent retention and transfer probes are missing. The
joint comparison is incomplete and therefore cannot pass. Among the seven
histories with transfer probes, only one has a positive energy difference
against either reset or cheap native-full. Do not treat a seven-history average
or a favorable matched steering comparison as a substitute for the full gate.

After acquisition, the learned, matched-native and matched-guarded arms have
30, 38 and 31 survivors respectively out of 192 original founders. All 30
learned-arm survivors executed both steering actions during acquisition; across
all learned founders there were 144,399 available midpoint decisions and
79,166 midpoint executions. Action support alone did not establish useful
learning, transfer or survival. A and B retention ratios are undefined because
the required C physical checkpoint is absent for one history.

The frozen protocol, captured sources, histories, complete traces, per-founder
action counts, available A/B/C anchors, checkpoints and results are in
`evidence/own-steering-development/20261026-v1`. Missing anchors were not
replaced. The independent collector readback verifies 833,408 decision/receipt
pairs, trace hashes, action counts, frozen weights and reported-work/storage/
payment ledgers; captured-source replay verifies 15 available A-to-B or B-to-C
checkpoint links. It does not independently reconstruct all operation counts,
sensory inputs or physical dynamics on the full study. The earlier synthetic
fixture audits remain distinct and do not remove those full-study limitations.

This is reused, outcome-informed development data, not fresh qualification.
Retention under functional pressure, original-ecology transfer, fresh audited
qualification and autonomous algorithm revision/search-productivity requirements
all remain unproved. Preserve this failure and all previously closed reserves.
