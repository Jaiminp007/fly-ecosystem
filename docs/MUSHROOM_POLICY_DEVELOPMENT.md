# Sparse dopamine-gated policy: prospective decision

The linear Expected SARSA screen, tile representation, and bounded Retrace development all retain their failed gates. The last change successfully produced multi-step replay without establishing B retention or survival. Another replay parameter search is not the selected next step.

I compared four directions before new performance observations. Direct policy plasticity removes the bootstrapped value target and greedy action boundary; sparse expansion and separate fast/slow synapses address shared-state credit and interference. A learned physical world model with planning could improve action evaluation, but it adds counterfactual-model error and planning costs before accessible prediction headroom has been established. A changed resource curriculum could make task structure more learnable, but a first circuit test on the existing schedule preserves comparability. Reward shaping could guide exploration, but would add an objective-design confound; the chosen circuit receives the same own net-energy change and is judged by unchanged net-energy, survival and separate skill gates. These are research judgments, not measured superiority of the chosen mechanism.

The biological motivation is limited and explicit. Sparse sensory expansion has been studied computationally in fly olfaction ([Dasgupta et al., 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29123069/)). Different mushroom-body dopamine compartments exhibit different memory dynamics ([Aso and Rubin, 2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4987137/)). Experiments also show timing-dependent bidirectional plasticity associated with dopamine receptors ([Handler et al., 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31230716/)). None of these papers establishes the policy-gradient rule, rates, transfer rule, or embodied performance used here. This is an abstract inspired algorithm, not a biological connectome or a claim about what real flies calculate.

Post-result source-review clarification: the repository already has a reward-modulated stochastic readout in `learning/trace-actor.mjs`, including eligibility and fast/slow sensory traces. This candidate is not the project's first direct policy learner. Its architecture adds a fixed sparse winner expansion and separates fast and slow **synaptic** weights; those are the distinctions tested against the recent value-learning methods.

The fixed candidate uses 64 sparse expansion cells. Each receives six fixed, distinct indices from the 39 non-bias coordinates of the existing normalized 40-coordinate current/history vector. A fixed xorshift seed generates connectivity before any observations; all bodies share this declared structure and each is charged for its stored copy and construction. Eight winner cells are active with value 1/sqrt(8), using deterministic index tie-breaking. Two 64-weight compartments sum to a policy logit. Fast-action probability is .05 + .9*sigmoid(logit), so both choices retain nonzero probability.

At each actual matured eight-tick transition, reward is the same own net-energy change divided by 120, including modeled costs. The baseline is an EMA with rate .05. The dopamine-like signal is clip((reward-baseline)/.01,-1,1). The policy-score factor is (chosenFast-p)*(.9*s*(1-s)/(p*(1-p))), with p the saved probability and s its saved sigmoid. Eligibility decays by exp(-duration/128)*.9 and adds that score factor times the sparse activity. Fast weights decay by exp(-duration/512), then receive .1*dopamine*eligibility, clipped to [-2,2]. At active synapses .02 of the resulting fast weight transfers into the slow compartment, with accepted transfer subtracted from fast weight so transfer alone preserves the summed readout. Slow weights are also bounded to [-2,2]. The baseline updates after plasticity. All numerical constants are fixed engineering design choices.

There are no future-value targets, action forks, oracle labels, or training from replay. A bounded sixteen-transition receipt ring supports factual recording and is storage-priced. The full fixed connectivity, two weight compartments, eligibility, history, pending record, baseline, random streams and accounting state are priced and checkpointed. Freeze stops both compartments, decay, baseline and transfer; reset clears all acquired weights, eligibility, history, baseline and receipts while retaining lifetime counters, fixed connectivity, and random streams. Read-only probes price prospective inference/state but never alter either compartment.

One fresh eight-history development bank reserves sixteen further histories. The candidate receives the same seven main arms and nine common-body probes. A matched fast-only circuit sets consolidation to zero and receives one main run plus nine development probes. The earlier linear learner and fixed feeding reflex are separate main references. This gives 224 development worlds and 304 possible confirmation worlds, a maximum of 528 worlds / 503,808 collection world-steps. Audits are repeat computation, not additional scientific units. Every prior registry and unused reserve is excluded before new seeds are generated.

Keep all twelve required energy contrasts: the original nine acquisition/initial-skill/separate retention/constant/incumbent comparisons, plus whole-sequence advantages over fast-only, old linear and reflex references. The optional thirteenth comparison concerns online eligibility. Development requires positive means for every required energy contrast, nonnegative means for all nine survival guards, and nonzero slow transfer plus nonzero dopamine in at least half of candidate updates in each history. Confirmation opens only after these gates and both full physical and independent arithmetic audits pass. It requires positive simultaneous Student-t lower bounds and exact paired sign-flip p<=.05/13 for each required contrast, plus the same survival/activity gates. No tuning, replacement, resampling or extension on either bank.

The memory remains fixed and shared across contexts; bounded storage alone does not establish distinct retained skills. Separate acquisition, earlier-skill-over-reset, and 95%-advantage retention gates must all pass. A successful screen would still leave persistent autonomous algorithm revisions, copied-improvement controls and causal gains in subsequent search to be demonstrated. A failed screen remains failed even if its circuit is biologically motivated.
