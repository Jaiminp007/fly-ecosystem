# Observe-arm pressure crossover

The fixed post-outcome crossover independently installs saved A/B taste and
steering models into the observe arm's eight D worlds. The A and B motor banks
are identical for all 61 D survivors. Every intervention pays the same 299
component-copy operations and preserves the same paid sensor, physical start,
debt and probe seed.

| B minus A component | A-probe mean energy / founder | B-probe mean energy / founder |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Taste, holding steering at A | -0.022615 | -0.006380 |
| Steering, holding taste at A | +0.116944 | +0.037397 |
| Taste–steering interaction | +0.017780 | +0.005863 |
| Both B versus both A | +0.112109 | +0.036880 |

The B steering change improves A in 7/8 of these reused total-world probes,
rather than showing the required transient A impairment. B's aggregate gain
is small and varies across histories. This supports an observed lack of
functional interference in this candidate, not a claim that there is no
possible interference or that the copied parameter change is solely caused by
B rewards. Competition, trajectories and RNG may diverge after intervention.

The next acquisition design must create and demonstrate genuinely competing
useful tasks or pressure without weakening the existing A/B skill, 5% transient
interference, 95% recovery, survival or transfer requirements. No synthetic
corruption, privileged phase labels, closed reserves or retrospective gate
changes are permitted. The earlier transfer signal remains development-only.

`evidence/observe-pressure-crossover/20261026-v1` contains captured protocol,
sources, starts, tapes and endpoints. Four focused tests pass. The independent
readback checks 488 assignments, 117,272 decision/receipt pairs, outcome
arithmetic and 64 exact captured-source replays. It is not an independent
physical simulator or a hardware-energy audit. Prior failures remain intact;
fresh qualification and subsequent autonomous revisions remain unproved.
