rbase A state, freezes the motor as originally planned, and changes B motor
execution only. The stop arm executes stationary on an accepted homogeneous
own contact; the shadow arm retains its sampled action. Both pay the same
declared selector work. Both use the shadow selector afterward. The source,
plan, starts, complete event tapes and endpoints are preserved in

The intervention increases factual example access and B/D survivors in these
evolving worlds, but both arms fail the A functional-pressure and net-intake
requirements. Both also miss the fixed six-of-eight exploratory transfer
consistency rule. The hand-coded stopping law is a diagnostic, not an acquired
skill; these reused development histories are not fresh qualification. The
differences in evolved survivor populations are not individual paired effects.

The independent ledger checker verifies 804,658 decision/receipt pairs,
49,549 own B examples, trace hashes, executed actions and reported-work,
storage, payments and unpaid debt. An additional outcome checker verifies
64,829 A-probe food/toxin receipts and pressure arithmetic. Captured-source
replay matches 256 complete event streams and ending checkpoints, 64 ancestry
links and 208 probe starts. These checks do not independently simulate physics
or establish complete low-level pricing adequacy.
