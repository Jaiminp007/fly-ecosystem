# Factual taste-contact provenance

Replay the existing cue-shared A, B and C acquisition checkpoints on the same
eight development histories using their captured sources. Check every emitted
event hash and final checkpoint against the original tapes. Instrument only by
reading the world during decision events; do not change the controller,
randomness, food or accounting. Restrict the descriptive census to the 56
founders surviving D, without treating them as an independent sample.

For each own stationary, accepting, odor-present contact with a nonzero own
`lastFood`, record the pre-action contact patches, selected nearest cue,
physical beneficial intake and toxin damage, and factual target. Partition
records by target agreement with selected-patch valence and by positive,
negative or mixed physical intake; compare pre-action nearby patch composition
and selected-patch amount. Separately match every retained D-reservoir record
to its own factual tick, including duplicates if any. Codebook and individual
patch valences are used only by the diagnostic observer, never for training.

The census is outcome-selected and post hoc. It does not establish which
physical mechanism caused each mismatch, whether a new observation is lawful
or affordable, whether changing credit improves learning, or whether any
qualification gate passes. Retain failed studies and do not open reserves.
