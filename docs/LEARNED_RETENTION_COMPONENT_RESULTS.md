# Learned-gate retention component intervention

The predeclared `LEARNED_RETENTION_COMPONENT_PLAN.md` crosses saved versus
D-current taste, steering and learned gate weights in the same reused D worlds.
For each component, numbers below are current minus saved energy per founder,
holding the other two components at either saved or current values.

| Probe | Taste at saved / current | Steering at saved / current | Gate at saved / current | All-current minus all-saved |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A | -0.005187 / +0.146433 | -0.036747 / +0.028362 | -0.264434 / -0.042216 | -0.123415 |
| B | -0.055838 / +0.072957 | -0.064589 / -0.001348 | -0.214690 / -0.162013 | -0.246806 |

On B probes, replacing the saved gate by the D-current gate reduces mean
energy at both other-component settings; each contrast is positive in only
1/8 histories. Mean net-intake changes for those gate contrasts are -0.028759
and -0.031354/founder. The B three-way energy interaction is +0.068092, and
other component effects depend on their settings. Thus the gate is a concrete
intervention target, not proof that gate drift alone explains retention loss;
changed trajectories, competition and random draws also matter. In A probes
the gate effects are less consistent and the three-way interaction is +0.073042.

Evidence: `evidence/learned-retention-components/20261026-v1`. Five focused
tests pass. An independent checker reconstructs 1,136 paid assignments,
verifies 268,107 decision/receipt pairs, ledger and contrast arithmetic, and
exactly replays all 128 arms. These are selected development worlds, not
independent physics, fresh qualification or proof of low-level pricing. The
original pressure, 95% retention, transfer, survival and later autonomous
revision gates remain unmet; all reserves and negative evidence remain intact.

Next test one phase-blind own-experience replay mechanism for the gate, versus
a matched same-buffer, same-draw, same-update-count current-sample control.
Both must learn only from their own executed actions and rewards, pay complete
memory/selection/update costs, and retain the original strong physical and
qualification controls. The diagnosis does not establish that replay will
improve retention or preserve transfer; predeclare before collecting.
