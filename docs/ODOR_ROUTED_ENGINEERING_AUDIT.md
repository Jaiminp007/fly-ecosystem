# Odor-routed curriculum engineering audit

The synthetic fixture runs three matched steering arms with four founders. One
founder has an artificially short lifespan, and the cue-to-odor-free phase
transition is deliberately moved to tick 40. These shortened phases are for
engineering coverage, not research performance or qualification.

A separate implementation reconstructs routing, actual motor choices, local
query membership, declared work increments, encoded storage, payments and debt
for 723 decision/receipt/observation triples. Each arm has 121 present-route and
120 absent-route decisions, three route switches and one terminal update.
Another implementation independently reconstructs 705 routed motor TD updates,
eligibility, reservoir selection and replay, including the three terminal
updates. Captured-source replay exactly reproduces each 40-tick cue checkpoint
and subsequent 40-tick odor-free continuation; it is not independent physics.

Seven synthetic 30-tick forks cover A current/earlier/reset and transfer
current/reset/native/guarded. Separate readbacks verify same-world hashes,
per-founder saved/reset banks, frozen inference, setup charges, storage, debt,
declared operation increments and 630 decision/receipt/observation triples.
The cheap native-full starting-world control is covered by the focused test,
not by those seven traced forks.

The captured sources, checkpoints, tapes and readbacks are in
`evidence/odor-routed-fixture/20261026-v1` and
`evidence/odor-routed-probe-fixture/20261026-v1`. These checks rely on logged
state and the existing abstract operation convention; they do not independently
simulate physics or establish general low-level cost adequacy. They do not
establish population-level action support, affordable learned skills, survival,
retention, transfer or autonomous improvement. Full-length matched development
and its own complete audits remain necessary, with closed reserves untouched.
