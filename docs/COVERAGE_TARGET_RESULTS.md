### Pricing caveat and working correction

Source review after collection finds the original taste prediction's repeated
log/sqrt expression needs 30 scalar operations, but its local allowance was 29.
Other allowances can be conservative; nevertheless this is not a complete
independent source-level operation audit and no exact-cost qualification claim
is made for v1. Frozen captured sources and failed outcomes are preserved.
The working module hoists these constants, charges two initialization operations
per body, and retains a conservative 29-operation prediction allowance (28
runtime arithmetic operations). This correction has not been rerun for outcomes
and does not rehabilitate the failed candidate. Transfer omits odor and therefore
does not call taste prediction; acquisition anchors still retain the original
study's pricing caveat.
