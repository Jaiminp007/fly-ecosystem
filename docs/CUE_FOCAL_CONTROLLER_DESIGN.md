# Fixed focal-controller survival diagnosis

Two outcome-selected D founders died in the residual-only world while cheap
native-full survived; both executed native steering throughout their recorded
residual-only lifetimes. This does not identify cost, other-body behavior or
RNG as the cause. Run one fixed four-arm total-world crossover for each selected
case: history 1172370180 founder 2 and history 1917475589 founder 2.

All arms begin at the same actual D world and original transfer seed. Start
with the same compacted, residual-only frozen cue-shared models and pay the
same 16 weight-mask writes. Every living body then pays a one-time sham or
switch setup charge equal to one declared scalar operation per encoded byte of
its pre-switch controller state. This explicitly prices the traversal needed
to replace a controller; it is not a measured machine-energy claim.

After setup, assign actual controllers as follows:

| Arm | Focal body | Other bodies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| all-residual | paid residual-only learner | paid residual-only learner |
| focal-cheap | cheap native-full | paid residual-only learner |
| focal-residual | paid residual-only learner | cheap native-full |
| all-cheap | cheap native-full | cheap native-full |

Cheap means the same native steering, full speed, always-accept command and
eight declared operations per step as the existing cheap native-full reference;
it does not compute or pay for the discarded learner or spatial selector.
Conversion preserves all already incurred, paid and outstanding acquisition,
probe and setup debt; it resets only the new controller's work counter. All
remaining live learners keep their real weights and costs. Dead founders and
their debt remain in the ledger. No cheap controller is described as a learned
skill, no fee is waived and no intervention outcome is used for training.

Save source/input hashes, full traces, per-body assignments and endpoints.
Compare focal survival and energy and complete-world totals across both
backgrounds. This is deliberately outcome-selected and changes programs,
work, future food competition and RNG; it is neither an isolated cost-only
effect nor evidence of learned policy qualification. Check exact restore,
native commands, same physical starts, setup/switch accounting and all ledger
debt. Original transfer, survival, functional-pressure, retention, fresh
qualification and RSI requirements remain unchanged.
