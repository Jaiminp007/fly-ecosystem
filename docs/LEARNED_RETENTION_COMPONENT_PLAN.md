# Learned-gate retention component intervention

For each of the eight reused histories, fork the learned-arm D world on the
original A and B probe seeds. Cross saved versus D-current taste, steering and
contact-gate weights in all eight frozen combinations, holding the D-world
support state, own action history, RNG, debt and A-frozen motor weights fixed.
For the A probe, saved means A; for the B probe, saved means B. Verify motor
weights are identical across all relevant anchors for each D survivor.

The first, second and third arm letters select taste, steering and gate,
respectively: `S` is saved and `C` is D-current. All arms pay the same 340
declared copy operations. Measure current-minus-saved effects for each
component at both all-saved and all-current settings, the full `CCC-SSS`
contrast, and their energy, net-intake and survivor outcomes. Interactions and
changed trajectories mean isolated contrasts do not identify individual
effects or sum to the total. Captured states and physical starts must match
across arms; preserve all existing support, prices and unpaid debt.

Record compressed starts, traces and ends with input and source hashes. An
independent checker must reconstruct every assignment and paid operation,
verify ledgers, probe arithmetic and exactly replay all event streams. This is
an outcome-informed diagnostic on reused development worlds, not a trained
learner revision, pressure qualification, independent physics or authorization
to open a reserve. Require at least 1 GiB free before collecting and a 100 MiB
floor; no threshold or component sweep beyond the fixed factorial is allowed.
