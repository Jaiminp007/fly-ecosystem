# Two-timescale factual replay: offline gate failed

Two fresh offline taste learners received the same 26,956 reconstructed own
stationary-contact examples from 122 D-surviving bodies across the original
and adjacent-conflict corpora. Both maintained the same 32-record factual
reservoir, eight-record recent queue, RNG draws, candidate calculations, two
updates per example and declared storage/work. Only the replay source differed.
The two corpora reuse the same eight history seeds and are not independent.

| Corpus | Mean B log-loss improvement | Positive histories | Mean A-at-C log-loss change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Original | +0.007774 | 5/8 | +0.012205 |
| Adjacent conflict | +0.012014 | 7/8 | +0.021211 |

Positive B improvement means lower B loss; positive A change means worse A
loss. The prespecified offline gate fails in both corpora: original misses
6/8, and A loss rises in both. This is an offline replay on selected factual
trajectories, not a physical intervention or a qualification result. Do not
promote the conflicting-corpus-only gain, tune the replay mixture on these
outcomes, or infer that these costs were paid by embodied organisms.

The first collection passed an unused phase field into the learner, violating
the planned input boundary. V1 and its readbacks remain preserved; V2 rejects
extra learner fields and strips phase at the boundary. The 16 V1/V2 result
rows are byte-identical, but V2 was rerun after outcomes and is not an
independent confirmation. See `docs/RECENCY_REPLAY_V1_BOUNDARY_NOTE.md`.

Four focused tests pass. V2's provenance readback verifies 48 source tapes,
187,392 selected-body decisions and all examples; its independent learning
readback reconstructs 107,824 updates, RNG, both stores, work, encoded state
and cue scores. Each arm reports approximately 3.06 million and 3.02 million
declared operations in the original and conflict corpora, respectively;
these are abstract offline counts, not physical energy charges. Evidence is
in `evidence/recency-replay-offline/20261026-v1` and
`evidence/recency-replay-offline/20261026-v2`. Prior failures, original
qualification and closed reserves remain intact.
