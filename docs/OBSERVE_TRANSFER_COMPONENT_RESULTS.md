# Observe-arm transfer contribution

The outcome-informed observe arm's existing transfer tapes contain 15,486
decisions, all at full speed and accepting food; 4,525 execute midpoint
steering. The frozen shared and odor-free residual components produce opposite
value-margin signs on 2,885 available decisions. Removing shared or residual
weights would change 1,697 or 1,188 commands respectively on those same
observations; those offline command comparisons are not physical effects.

All model interventions pay the same 16 component writes. The original reset
and cheap controls retain their own recorded setup costs, and their physical
starting worlds are hash-matched. These are total-world effects on reused D
bodies; competition, trajectories and RNG can change. The full model's
exploratory transfer signal survives this matched diagnostic. Component masks
are selected after outcomes and are not trained policies, and these comparisons
do not resolve the failed functional-pressure requirement or establish fresh
qualification. Do not treat the residual-only post-outcome threshold as a pass.
