# Descriptive exposure and credit after the failed tick study

Continuation: [conditional policy and consumed-patch provenance](TICK_POLICY_DIAGNOSIS.md)
now independently measures saved model outputs on fixed recorded contexts and
exactly replays consumed patch identities. It identifies widespread stationary
low-amount sensor/intake mismatch in this failed bank, without claiming a causal
effect on qualification or a successful remedy. Read that newer record first.

This is a post-hoc description of the existing failed bank, not a new test,
failure-cause conclusion, model selection or parameter-tuning authorization.
The original development failure and all closed reserves are unchanged.
Independent arithmetic and full exact replay of the study are recorded in
[the campaign record](TICK_LEARNING_RESULTS.md); check its latest audit status.

The source-bound supplemental `describe-tick-development.py` streams the
candidate-only events from all eight recorded compressed tapes, verifies every
uncompressed hash and cross-checks decision/update counts and total beneficial,
harmful and toxin amounts against the frozen rows. It opens no simulator or new
seed. The output is `supplemental/exposure-credit-description.json` in the study.

Transition phase is assigned by the recorded decision tick that started its
one-tick interval. Phase-end decisions may mature at the next choice in the next
resource regime; the last 29 surviving-body decisions remain pending at the final
stop. Those boundaries explain why decision and transition totals differ at the
last phase. No synthetic terminal reward was inserted.

Odor was present on 97,952 A decisions, 97,218 B decisions and every C/return-A
decision. Zero recorded candidate physical receipts contained both beneficial
and harmful intake. Thus the earlier engineering fixture showing mixed intake
does not establish its prevalence or causal role in this failed study. It also
does not eliminate movement-related cue/outcome mismatch, which this count
does not measure.

Beneficial versus harmful intake was 1,398.3523 / 1,389.5204 in A,
1,365.0934 / 1,333.0538 in B, 818.5793 / 807.1210 in C and 190.5403 / 165.0102
in return-A. Actual toxin damage was clipped by available body energy in some
later receipts, as in the engine. Background plus policy physical costs were
4,206.7266, 4,193.8210, 3,429.3481 and 570.3992 respectively. Positive food
intake need not yield a positive net-energy transition.

Action counts in [accept-cruise, refuse-cruise, accept-stationary,
refuse-stationary] order were [47,042,17,402,17,010,16,850] in A,
[46,679,17,201,17,225,17,037] in B, [38,671,14,091,14,263,14,349] in C and
[6,737,2,489,2,436,2,452] in return-A. Aggregate proportions are close to the
disclosed initialization probabilities [.475,.175,.175,.175]. This does **not**
prove unchanged conditional policies, absent cue learning, an optimal action
distribution or why the acquired-skill gate failed. All 192 original candidate
bodies did change parameters, satisfying the activity check without establishing
useful behavior.

The next diagnosis must distinguish the embodied association/credit opportunity
from the actual learning updates' functional effect. No rate sweep, hindsight
oracle selection, easier survival requirement, reuse of this bank as acceptance
evidence or opening its sixteen-history reserve follows from these descriptions.
An eventual new candidate requires a new substantive hypothesis, fresh locked
histories and the same honest acquired-skill/retention/transfer standards.
