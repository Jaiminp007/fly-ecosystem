# Bounded stall and escape diagnostic

This replaces the general pause-fork proposal after the read-only Astra/root
discussion in [the review thread](t3-thread://v1/mcp:65dee2c2-6de9-411b-9e34-31afe7486364).
It is exploratory, uses only attempt-15's eight reusable end-C anchors and original
transfer seeds, and cannot qualify learned transfer or RSI. Previous failed timing
results remain unchanged. No learner variant or duration search is authorized by
this design.

Each factual transfer trajectory uses the same frozen shadow learner and
half-speed approach cruise for 256 ticks, with identically shaped paid selector
and pulse state. Completed observation work is settled immediately, including
surviving bodies; the earlier tiny final-unbilled-work omission is not changed in
old evidence. Raw food senses, previous intake and local contact remain the only
information available for action/event selection. Observer-only reconstruction
uses native Spatial candidate order to identify selected targets, counts deep
obstacle-interior targets, and reconstructs collision rejection from actual
normal draws without advancing the RNG. Movement reconstruction must match the
real positions. Observer instrumentation must reproduce uninstrumented physical
trajectories and charged controller state exactly.

There are two fixed one-based transfer-decision windows: **17--112** and
**129--224**. In each, select the earliest pre-step checkpoint where a living body
has sixteen consecutive preceding decisions with sensed food (`raw[3]>0`) and
nonpositive previous intake (`raw[13]<=0`), plus a wall warning (`raw[10]>0` or
`raw[11]>0`) on at least one of its preceding four decisions. Break simultaneous
ties by smallest founder ID. Missing windows remain missing. This selects at
most sixteen checkpoints without looking at outcomes, patch IDs or geometry.

At each event, all other bodies continue cruising. The focal body either cruises
(C), performs sixteen stationary-accept actions (P), or performs sixteen native
refuse-cruise actions (D), then returns to approach cruise. D changes both steering
and feeding; it is not a pure steering intervention. Its primitive already exists
but is unavailable to the current forced-accept absent-odor transfer scaffold.
Any subsequent executable learner using it must declare expanded transfer
authority and retain matched controls.

Restore the full already-initialized pre-step checkpoint, without compaction,
relocation, refill or revival. Keep all original founders, physical/policy RNG
states, budgets and inherited debt. Both pulse fields are reserved/paid in C as
well as P/D. Real extra refuse-motor work is charged. C outcomes can be reused
from the factual reference; one exact no-intervention restoration must reproduce
its final full checkpoint. Differing deaths may alter subsequent shared-RNG draw
assignment; do not claim identical shocks for each individual afterward.

Measure at pulse end (16 elapsed ticks), 64 elapsed ticks, and the original
transfer endpoint. Missing horizons beyond extinction retain the extinct endpoint.
Report all signed energy/debt/survivor differences per original 24 founders;
separate focal-body and other-body budget contributions, intake, charged physical
costs, target persistence and collision exposure. Aggregate within history and
window; the event forks are not independent histories, and their gains cannot be
added to claim a realizable repeated policy. Never use a hindsight max as a
deployable policy or training label.

Few eligible stalls or little deep-interior target exposure weakens this proposed
bottleneck. P comparable to D leaves timing plausible. Durable cohort benefit
from D while P fails motivates a separately declared lawful escape-policy test;
focal-only improvement or early gains lost by the endpoint do not meet that bar.
Failure rejects only these particular interventions and sampled states, not all
steering policies. Qualification standards remain unchanged.
