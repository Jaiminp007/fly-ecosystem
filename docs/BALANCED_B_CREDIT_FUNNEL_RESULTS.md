Among zero-example survivors, the earliest unreached nested stage is no
stationary decision for 6/26 rbase and 6/28 blend; no contact after a
stationary accepted action for 13/26 and 17/28; and no homogeneous contact
after such an action for 7/26 and 5/28. The last category includes only
observed contact with differing cue patterns, not inferred hidden valence.
The census does not say whether these agents encountered homogeneous contact
while moving, nor whether any alternative action would have improved outcomes.

Evidence: `evidence/balanced-b-credit-funnel/20261026-v1`. The independent
checker reconstructs 58,880 own decisions, 8,092 examples, saved `seen`
differences and trace hashes. This is selected, reused-world observation; it
does not independently simulate physics or prove exposure causality. Do not
use these groups as qualification samples or relax the original gates.

Before choosing a physically paid action intervention, count whether the
zero-example survivors encountered homogeneous contacts under *any* motor action
and whether they accepted at those moments. If such opportunities exist, a
fixed matched sensor-based stopping witness could test reachable credit
headroom; if not, local movement/target access needs investigation instead.
Any compiled witness is a diagnostic control, not an acquired skill or proof
of retention, transfer or autonomous revision.
