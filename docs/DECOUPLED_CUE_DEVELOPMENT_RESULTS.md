The candidate **fails the unchanged transfer consistency gate**. Its raw,
outcome-informed A/B probes show +0.084227 A interference per founder,
+0.026587 B acquisition contrast and +0.021836 A net-intake contrast; the
reported 5%-of-uncorrected-A-advantage excess is +0.019806. Descriptive A/B
retention ratios are 104.87% and 100.04%. These are not confidence bounds,
reset-initialization-neutral qualification effects, or a fresh functional
pressure pass. The shared-only control meets the reused-history exploratory
transfer rule but its pressure contrasts are negative. No arm satisfies the
joint research objective. D survivors are 62/192 candidate and 60/192 control.

The prior offline screen passed its fixed rule, but this physical study shows
why offline prediction is insufficient. Twelve immediate focused tests pass.
The main readback verifies 958,488 decision/receipt pairs, 128 checkpoint links,
208 probe starts and reported ledgers; the independent outcome check covers
60,492 A-probe receipts. A separate acquisition replay reconstructs 43,759
own factual examples, decoupled weights and RNG, 562,220 decisions, 335,963
target scores, sensor work, actual action credit and encoded state across 64
exact source replays. These checks do not independently simulate physics or
establish complete low-level hardware-energy adequacy.
