# Extended-B energy and taste census

The failed extended-B tapes show energy depletion, rather than lifespan, as
the dominant recorded death cause. Across eight histories per arm, B has 119
observe and 121 filter energy deaths and no age deaths. During C, observe has
72 energy and one age death; filter has 66 energy and one age death. These are
the simulator's recorded causes, not causal interventions on mortality.

| B physical ledger, eight histories | Observe | Filter |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Starting body energy | 7,801.02 | 7,807.80 |
| Beneficial intake | 2,234.68 | 2,134.09 |
| Toxin damage | 2,242.72 | 2,186.68 |
| Total physical cost | 7,270.45 | 7,259.71 |
| Of which paid extra learner cost | 458.29 | 462.08 |
| Ending body energy | 522.52 | 495.50 |

On these recorded trajectories, beneficial intake approximately cancels toxin
damage while physical costs drain the starting energy. Paid extra cost is a
subset of total cost, about 6.3% in B; subtracting it arithmetically from the
recorded cost does not establish a counterfactual outcome, since actions,
competition and survival could change. C starts with little aggregate body
energy and also has negative recorded net intake. No claim about sole causation
or actual hardware energy follows.

For actual unambiguous local-contact decisions, evaluator-only codebook labels
show the following mean feeding propensities:

| Arm | First B half, good / bad | Second B half, good / bad |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Observe | 0.832 / 0.411 | 0.879 / 0.297 |
| Filter | 0.822 / 0.397 | 0.882 / 0.252 |

This is descriptive cue discrimination, not proof of useful causal learning:
later observations come from survivors, decisions repeat, and movement and
overlap mean the cue need not identify all food consumed. It argues against
assuming that the long-B failure is merely absence of a taste signal. A useful
next intervention must address affordable net energy and mortality while still
demonstrating real A/B pressure and satisfying the unchanged original schedule
and transfer controls; simply removing the accounted learner fee is not a
measured rescue.

The physical-ledger census and readback are in
`evidence/extended-b-energy-census/20261026-v1`; the independent taste readback
reconstructs 335,858 decisions/receipts and 141,323 selected local contacts in
`evidence/extended-b-taste-census/20261026-v1`. Both use original trace hashes
and checkpoints, not an independent simulator. Closed reserves and all prior
failures remain intact.
