# Full-speed acquisition: prospective development design

October 26, 2026. The alternative-target witness failed and does not justify a
learned version of its ranking law. Separately, the factual acquisition tapes
prove that the old motor learner never executes the full-speed benchmark action.
Engineer a distinct speed-learning core that removes that support mismatch,
without changing the world or weakening the bare native-full comparison.

## Physical integration and one declared development run

The separate wrapper is learning/coverage-experiment.mjs. The engine remains
unchanged. Native steering is preserved exactly. Feeding uses an eleven-feature
shared quadratic taste head with log(3) prior, scaled by sqrt(11), a .5 signed
intake logistic update and one replay from a 32-record own-example reservoir.
Only actual stationary accepting actions with pre-action contact and nonzero own
signed intake provide taste labels. Moving intake is never assigned a stationary
label. Feed probability is .05+.9*prediction, or exactly accept without odor.
All observations use the priced existing contact encoder. The motor's pending
action is always the actual executed speed, including explicit fixed controls.

Use the same eight reusable development histories, not fresh qualification seeds.
Acquire A/B/C for 512 ticks each under candidate, unlearned (frozen initially),
frozen (at B), reset (at B and C), and cheap native-full. No learner sweep. Fork
candidate anchors after A and C into frozen current, reset, and native-full on
the same 256-tick return-A and original-ecology transfer resources. All forks
preserve physical state and inherited unpaid debt. Freeze/reset setup operations
are charged against the pre-intervention debt baseline, not forgiven. The
architecture-matched reset uses the same learning/feeding machinery with zero
weights and greedy full-speed ties. Native-full discards the learner but retains
all inherited debt, accepts and executes full speed.

The exploratory gate is positive mean energy, at least six positive histories
out of eight and nonnegative summed survivor differences against BOTH reset and
native-full on transfer. Report original-task acquisition/retention alongside,
but this gate alone cannot establish retention under functional pressure,
qualification, autonomous revision or RSI. A failed gate closes this precise
candidate; no post-outcome tuning is licensed by this protocol.

Save complete source/input hashes before outcomes, all candidate A/B/C anchors,
complete decision/receipt/observation tapes, final checkpoints and row outcomes.
Use exact continuation and actual-action provenance tests before the run. Meter
controller arithmetic and encoded state including reservoirs, transient pending
records, all RNGs and outstanding debt. Observer/runner evidence is not retained
as free policy state. Reusable development is not fresh confirmation.

The three physical commands are stationary (-1), half speed (0) and full speed
(1). All are available during acquisition AND transfer. A fixed 10% exploration
mixture gives each action nonzero probability during acquisition; exact greedy
ties favor the existing full-speed reference, not an artificial impairment.
No phase identifier, body geometry, hidden food identity or future label enters.
Thirteen fixed shared features use existing food direction/proximity, energy,
walls, own previous intake and lawful contact presence, plus three interactions.

One-step Expected SARSA(lambda) learns from the executed action's actual own
budget-adjusted energy difference, divided by 120. Discount is exp(-1/256),
lambda .9, live rate .05, one replay rate .01, weights bounded by 8. A 32-record
uniform reservoir contains only that body's own actual transitions. No fork
outcome or proximity-sign proxy is a reward. Meter all scalar arithmetic and
retain the normal storage/debt ledger for later physical integration.

Live updates wait for the next lawful observation; terminal receipts update
immediately with zero bootstrap. Freezing/resetting explicitly counts and drops
the one boundary transition that has not received a next observation; do not
invent a next sensor value or claim it was learned. Final uncharged work must
be billed in the world wrapper. Frozen inference cannot change weights.

This core is NOT integrated performance, a successful alternative-target
learner, functional-pressure evidence or qualification. Before any such claim,
integrate a separate lawful taste/feeding head, synchronize executed speed and
feeding credit, carry all old costs/debt, check restore/freeze/reset/extinction,
and demonstrate actual action coverage per founder. Compare against matched
unlearned/frozen/reset and unchanged bare full-speed policies. Reused development
is labeled tuning; fresh locked qualification and every RSI stage remain later.
