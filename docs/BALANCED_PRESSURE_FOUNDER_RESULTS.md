# Balanced replay founder pressure census

The fixed `BALANCED_PRESSURE_FOUNDER_PLAN.md` census decomposes saved-A versus
saved-B total-world probe outcomes by each D survivor's factual B taste-example
count. A pressure means saved A minus saved B; B acquisition reverses that
direction. Energy values are totals across selected survivors, not per-founder
independent causal estimates.

| Arm / group | D survivors | A energy total | A net-intake total | B energy total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Rbase, at least one B example | 34 | +0.335346 | -1.009534 | -1.133361 |
| Rbase, zero B examples | 26 | -11.965483 | -12.146101 | +4.784105 |
| Blend, at least one B example | 27 | -7.751157 | -7.872330 | +9.464292 |
| Blend, zero B examples | 28 | +1.887434 | +1.896394 | -4.346457 |

The grouping reveals heterogeneous contributions, not a consistent exposure
effect across arms. Nearly half of these D survivors received **no factual B
taste examples**: 26/60 in rbase and 28/55 in blend. This is an important
observation-coverage constraint, but the groups are selected by later survival
and own experience and share interacting physical worlds. The contrasts can
reflect movement, competition, RNG and other bodies; they do not prove what B
exposure caused or establish a passing pressure gate.
