# Component mortality and cost census

The post-outcome same-start census traces all 56 bodies entering the eight
residual-only, full, matched-native and cheap native-full transfer worlds.
Residual-only ends with 51 survivors versus 53 for cheap native-full. The
three age deaths are the same founders in both; the two discordant founders
die of energy depletion under residual-only and survive under cheap native.

| History / founder | Residual-only death | Residual midpoint actions | Cheap-native intake | Residual intake |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1172370180 / 2 | tick 1977 | 0 / 185 | 15.234 | 4.692 |
| 1917475589 / 2 | tick 1875 | 0 / 83 | 28.856 | 0.224 |

Both lost founders actually executed native steering throughout their
residual-only lifetimes. The matched forced-native world also loses both
(ticks 1977 and 1874); the full model saves the first but not the second.
This rules out an account requiring those founders to have personally executed
a midpoint action in the residual-only world. It does **not** establish why
cheap native survives: other bodies steer differently, pay different costs,
consume food and alter RNG trajectories. The large intake differences cannot
be interpreted as isolated individual counterfactual effects.

Across the 56 initially live founders, recorded paid extra was 32.754 units in
residual-only versus 0.061 in cheap native; total recorded cost already
includes paid extra. Lifetimes differ, so these are not per-tick matched causal
cost estimates. Residual-only's initial charge ledger also includes paid model
copy, compaction and mask setup that cheap native avoids; inherited acquisition
debt was not erased. Earlier positive energy totals and the -2 survivor guard
remain unchanged. The result motivates a separately specified focal/other-body
and controller-cost intervention, not a retrospective threshold or a waiver of
the real budget.

Hashed inputs, complete per-founder receipt summaries and last-decision records
are in `evidence/cue-component-mortality/20261026-v1`; the census independently
checks trace hashes and receipt balances, not the simulator's physical dynamics.
The component mask is not promoted, and all qualification reserves stay closed.
