# Supported pressure paired-prefix diagnosis

The fixed comparison in `SUPPORTED_PRESSURE_PREFIX_PLAN.md` examines saved-A
versus saved-B probes from the same protect D worlds and seeds. All 16 probe
pairs start with identical recorded physical worlds.

| Probe family | First differing output: taste | First differing output: steering |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| A | 7/8 histories | 1/8 |
| B | 4/8 histories | 4/8 |

No pair's first recorded difference is a motor output, requested extra charge,
input or receipt. Among the seven A-probe first taste differences, five occur
with stationary motor commands; the other two occur while moving. These are
first **controller-output** differences, not necessarily first consequential
physical differences: a changed accept command may not produce consumption.
First divergence does not quantify its effect on endpoint energy or rule out
later steering and interaction effects, and the comparison is outcome-informed
reused development evidence, not a qualification result.

Evidence: `evidence/supported-pressure-prefix/20261026-v1`. A separate checker
reconstructs all 16 paired prefixes and verifies all 173,298 complete trace
events against their stored hashes. It does not independently simulate the
world or attribute endpoint effects to a component.

This motivates, but cannot replace, the paid A/B taste-by-steering factorial in
`SUPPORTED_PRESSURE_FACTORIAL_PLAN.md`. The collector remains unrun because the
workspace volume is below its 1 GiB free-space preflight; preserve evidence and
do not bypass that guard. All scientific gates and closed reserves remain.
