## The anatomy (composed)

1. Capability: proposals fire, sandboxes evaluate, votes carry —
   demonstrated 9×. The loop is a working internal actuator.
2. Operators: surprise transfers (+0.07–0.16 over static, 4 lines);
   gain/skip/damage/threat do not (fresh or transplanted).
3. Adoption: unanimous when the operator is good (nothing to
   select); split when the set is mixed (majority wins).
4. Alignment: no eval objective available in-agent predicts
   transfer (train-error misaligned; flip-eval too strict/untested
   vs standard on the same bank).
5. Barren banks exist (463xxx, 464xxx: nothing passes 5%) —
   adoptability varies by history, unlicensed to screen.
