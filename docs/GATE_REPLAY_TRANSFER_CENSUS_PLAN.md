# Gate-replay transfer trajectory census

On each reused fresh and reservoir D world, compare the already recorded
transfer-current and transfer-native-steering probes from the same physical
start and seed. Reconstruct each D survivor's actual decisions, midpoint
availability and executed steering, wall-contact sensor, physical receipts,
paid work and endpoint survival/energy in each evolved world. Report total
intake, toxin, physical cost, unpaid debt and budget-adjusted energy contrasts
per original founder, plus discordant survivors and per-body distributions.
Validate identical physical starts, every decision/receipt pair, receipt energy
arithmetic, endpoint and trace hashes; an independent readback must recompute
the totals and signs.

Steering is a physical whole-world intervention but trajectories, other bodies,
random streams and lifetimes diverge. Per-body pairs and post-outcome groups
are descriptive, not fixed-neighbor causal effects. Counts of food or cost can
change because of survival; do not use counterfactual labels in learners, open
reserves, tune criteria or claim fresh qualification or independent physics.
