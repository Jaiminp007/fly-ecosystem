# MATE-WINDOW DEPLOYMENT DECISION: licensed optional, never default

Decision record (policy, not a plan — no worlds spent, no code
changed, no defaults touched). Discharges the V2 verdict's
"deployment specified separately" item. Status: FINAL unless a
future line overturns it with separately-frozen rationale.

## Decision

**Early-window enrichment is LICENSED as a named optional arm
(`earlyWindow`: enriched phase-0 only, 5.0, else legacy) in
future mating instruments. It is NOT deployed as a default.
Baselines stay legacy (null); engine null-default untouched.**

## Reasons

1. **Comparability is the currency.** Frozen inference
   (identical baselines across lines) is load-bearing for
   every cross-line reading in the map (transfer taxes,
   hub contrasts, gain effects). Default-enrichment would
   rebase all future controls (every "control" enriched in
   phase 0) and break comparability with 8000+ worlds of
   legacy-baseline evidence. Not spent for births.
2. **CONFIG_AUDIT_V1's lesson cuts against defaults.**
   Enrichment inflates LEVELS (birthy arms were enriched,
   not just hub/proximal). A default-enriched substrate
   would corrupt all future level readings (every arm
   birthy-looking, topology/learning effects masked by
   the enriched floor). The confound, once named, is not
   re-installed.
3. **The mechanism is real and available.** V2 GO (early 28
   > control 16, late == control seed-identical) proves
   timing, not just amount. Lines where births are the
   binding constraint (void-risk designs) may license the
   arm to convert INCOMPLETEs into tests. Availability
   without imposition: the middle path.
4. **Null-default discipline intact.** No engine change
   (nutrientOverride default null); no runner change;
   no scorer change. The license is exercised per-line
   by naming the arm in a frozen plan (with its own
   window gates) — never by altering shared defaults.

## Conditions of use (binding on future lines)

- C1: pre-register window gates (manipulation + timing +
  reference, MATE-WINDOW G2–G4 form) whenever the arm is
  used; legacy control mandatory alongside (no enriched-
  only designs — the contrast is the point).
- C2: report enriched-vs-legacy level shift explicitly
  (absolute births both arms; never report the enriched
  arm's level as a topology/learning effect).
- C3: no stacking with other enrichment arms in the same
  line without a no-enrichment control (confound ceiling:
  at most one enrichment dimension varies per line).

## Status

- Decided: CONDITIONAL LICENSE (this doc). No code, no
  worlds, no defaults changed. Future lines cite this
  decision when licensing the arm; deviations need their
  own frozen rationale.
