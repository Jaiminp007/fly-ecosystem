# Motor-only freeze: outcome-informed development intervention

All eight motor-only freeze continuations start from the actual learned A
checkpoints, whose learned and frozen-after-A recorded prefixes match. Freezing
both routed motor banks while taste and steering continue training leaves 62 of
192 founders alive at D, versus 20 in continuously trained and 60 in fully
frozen-after-A arms. At A/B/C/D boundaries the motor-freeze survivors are
192/183/70/62; continuing survivors are 192/186/30/20.

Relative to continuous training, the fixed intervention gains +7.430442
budget-adjusted energy per original founder on average, positive in 8/8 reused
histories, and has 42 more survivors. Relative to fully frozen-after-A it gains
+0.300940 on average, positive in 3/8, with two more survivors. This is a
post-outcome total-world intervention. It simultaneously disables motor updates
and motor exploration and changes motor work, later movement, resource
competition and RNG trajectories. It does not isolate gradient updates or
prove an acquired skill; no fork outcome becomes a training label.

Independent readback checks the common A anchors, 80-operation setup, actual
frozen motor inference, unchanged motor weights and reported-work/storage/debt
for 181,816 decision/receipt pairs. It does not independently simulate physics
or price every low-level operation. Captured sources, input hashes, starts,
full continuation tapes, finals and readback are in
`evidence/odor-routed-motor-freeze/20261026-v1`.

## Fixed follow-on probes

For each existing motor-freeze continuation, replay the captured start to recover
its actual B and C checkpoints and verify the final D checkpoint exactly. On the
same D physical bodies, compare frozen A and B saved models, frozen D model and
reset on the original cue tasks. On original-ecology transfer compare frozen D
current, reset, matched native, matched guarded and cheap native-full from the
same physical state and the pre-existing separate transfer seed. Preserve all
costs and debt and do not fabricate a missing checkpoint.

The fixed transfer development gate remains positive mean energy, at least 6/8
positive histories, and nonnegative aggregate survivor difference against both
reset and cheap native-full. A/B descriptive retention requires positive saved
advantage and complete support; the 95% condition and functional pressure are
not relaxed. These are again reused, outcome-selected development histories;
even a passing diagnostic would not establish fresh qualification or autonomous
revision/search productivity.
