# Challenge evaluation

24 age-zero, energy-matched agents; paired positions/seeds; descendants sampled in saved live-agent order, repeated only if fewer than 24. Acquired neural state reset. Mutation and reproduction remain enabled. 7200-tick horizon. Small descriptive comparison, not proof of intelligence or isolated neural improvement; inherited body/life-history traits also differ.

All recorded cohorts survived the requested horizon. Birth counts favor descendants in these runs, including with lifetime learning disabled. This small experiment does not establish a learning advantage or isolate the contribution of neural structure. The donor population was evolved in the original habitat, not this challenge schedule.
