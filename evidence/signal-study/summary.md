# Signal ablation comparison

Ten matched seeds compared normal signaling with disabled and deterministically scrambled signals for 7200 ticks. Reproduction, mutation, and learning remained enabled; seasonality and the scheduled challenges were off. Each treatment began with identical founder genomes and positions.

- Normal minus disabled final population: -4.4 agents (95% t interval -12.15 to 3.35).
- Normal minus scrambled final population: -1.7 agents (95% t interval -8.59 to 5.19).
- Food eaten differences were also uncertain; both 95% intervals include zero.

These runs do not establish a receiver benefit, cooperation, or signaling intent. Signal energy cost and exposure per agent tick are in `results.json`, along with each seed and source hashes.
