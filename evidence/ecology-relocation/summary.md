# Seeded food relocation comparison

Ten paired seeds compared stable renewal centers to seeded relocations over 7200 ticks. Both arms retain reproduction, mutation, and learning.

- Mean population: baseline 86.2, relocation 61.4.
- Paired mean population difference: -24.83 (95% t interval -31.18 to -18.48).
- This is a descriptive ecological pressure comparison. Evolution and lifetime learning remain enabled; it cannot isolate a neural learning effect or establish stable niche formation.

See results.json for seed-level outcomes and source hashes.
